# SOP: Certificar Aliado
**Versión**: 1.1 | **Última actualización**: 2026-03-24 | **Propietario**: Delivery Manager + VP Aliados

---

## 1. Propósito

Transferir sistemáticamente la metodología, sales playbooks, y delivery processes de MetodologIA a aliado, asegurando que partner puede ejecutar sin supervisión a nivel de >80% quality. Resultado: Certificado formal que habilita co-selling y revenue.

---

## 2. Supuestos Explícitos

1. **Transferencia de IQ requiere 40-60 horas de inmersión estructurada**: No es posible aprender metodología MetodologIA mediante autodidacta o documentos solos; requiere aprendizaje activo + feedback iterativo.

2. **Certificación es binaria**: Aliado es certificado o no certificado; no hay "partial certification" que permite co-selling. Gray area resuelto mediante "supervised deals" hasta pasar.

3. **Cuatro módulos son necesarios y suficientes**: Metodología core, sales playbook, delivery execution, compliance/governance. Omitir uno = introducir riesgo en otra parte.

4. **Las exámenes deben tener pass rate de 70-80%**: Si aliado group tiene <70% pass rate, módulo está mal diseñado; si >80%, es demasiado fácil. Iteración on difficulty.

5. **Co-ejecución de 1+ deal en vivo es requerida**: Pasar examen no garantiza que aliado puede ejecutar; necesitamos evidencia de ejecución real, no simulated.

6. **Certificación es predicate para Gate H-01 (Delivery Handoff)**: Si aliado no está certificado, cliente handoff is blocked. Sin excepciones.

---

## 3. Ámbito

### Incluido
- 4 módulos de transferencia de IQ (16+12+16+4 = 48h)
- Exámenes de conocimiento
- Shadow + co-ejecución de deals
- Feedback + coaching
- Certificado emitido
- Documentation de aprobación

### Excluido
- Ongoing training (post-certification)
- Technical tool training (e.g., Salesforce, Slack)
- Marketing/brand co-development
- Deal-specific coaching (that's VP Ventas job)

---

## 4. Fases y Responsabilidades

### Pre-Requisito: Fit Evaluation PASSED
**Entrada**: Aliado with score ≥70% from evaluar-fit-aliado-sop
**Without this**: Do not start onboarding

---

### Fase 1: MÓDULO 1 - METODOLOGÍA CORE (16h, Semana 1-2)
**Propietario**: Delivery Manager | **Format**: 4 x 4h sessions (weekly)

**Contenido**:
1. MetodologIA origin story & philosophy (1h)
   - Why we built it; differentiation vs market
   - Core principles: customer-centric, data-driven, iterative
2. 5 Core pillars of methodology (3h deep dive each):
   - Pilar 1: Discovery & Scoping (customer outcomes, scope definition, success metrics)
   - Pilar 2: Strategy & Alignment (organizational buy-in, leadership alignment, governance)
   - Pilar 3: Execution & Change Management (team enablement, process design, adoption)
   - Pilar 4: Measurement & Iteration (KPIs, feedback loops, optimization)
   - Pilar 5: Sustainability (scaling, documentation, knowledge transfer)
3. Vertical-specific adaptations (3h)
   - How methodology adapts to different industries/company sizes
   - Case studies: 2-3 successful engagements per vertical
4. Q&A + discussion (2h)

**Assessment**:
- **Attendance**: 100% (must attend all 4 sessions)
- **Participation**: Notes, questions, case study discussions documented
- **Exam**: Theoretical exam (40 questions, 30min, >70% pass)
  - Mix of definition, scenario, application questions
  - No open-book

**Output**: Exam score + attendance record + feedback from Delivery Manager

---

### Fase 2: MÓDULO 2 - SALES PLAYBOOK (12h, Semana 2-3)
**Propietario**: VP Ventas + Delivery Manager | **Format**: 3 x 4h sessions

**Contenido**:
1. Sales conversation framework (2h)
   - How to discover MetodologIA-fit opportunities
   - Qualification criteria (what is/isn't a fit for us)
   - Discovery conversation structure & key questions
2. Proposal development & positioning (2h)
   - How to structure proposal using MetodologIA methodology
   - Pricing + packaging logic
   - Objection handling: budget, timeline, in-house capability
3. Sales playbook by vertical (6h)
   - Vertical 1: Target customers, pain points, success stories (2h)
   - Vertical 2: (2h)
   - Vertical 3: (2h)
   - [Adapt based on aliado's focus verticals]
4. Tools & templates walkthrough (2h)
   - Proposal templates, discovery decks, ROI calculator
   - CRM integration for pipeline tracking

**Assessment**:
- **Attendance**: 100%
- **Role-play exercise**: 2x simulated sales conversations (discovery + proposal), graded by VP Ventas
  - Criteria: Proper qualification, listening, positioning, handling objections
  - Score: Pass/Fail per conversation (Fail = repeat role-play)
- **Knowledge exam**: 30 questions on playbook, >70% pass

**Output**: Role-play score + exam score

---

### Fase 3: MÓDULO 3 - DELIVERY PROCESS (16h, Semana 3-5)
**Propietario**: Delivery Manager | **Format**: 4 x 4h sessions + shadow + co-exec

**Contenido**:
1. Delivery end-to-end workflow (4h)
   - Phase 1: Kickoff & Planning (customer alignment, team setup, success metrics)
   - Phase 2: Execution (design/build/test cycles, quality gates)
   - Phase 3: Enablement & Handoff (training, documentation, support transition)
   - Phase 4: Post-delivery (measurement, expansion opportunities)
2. Quality standards & SLAs (2h)
   - Delivery quality framework (what constitutes "done")
   - Service level agreements: response time, escalation, support hours
   - Risk management: what to escalate to MetodologIA
3. Tools & processes (2h)
   - Project management tool (Asana, Monday, etc)
   - Document management & templating
   - Communication & reporting cadence
4. Case study deep-dives (2h)
   - 2x full case studies walked through step-by-step
   - Lessons learned, common pitfalls, best practices
5. Shadow phase (variable, 8-16h over 2-3 weeks)
   - Aliado shadows 1-2 active engagements
   - Observes kickoff, execution sessions, stakeholder calls
   - Documents observations, asks questions
6. Co-execution phase (variable, 8-16h over 2-3 weeks)
   - Aliado leads 1 engagement with Delivery Manager shadowing/coaching
   - Weekly retrospectives on execution quality

**Assessment**:
- **Attendance**: 100%
- **Shadow report**: Written summary of 2-3 observations from shadowing (min 500 words)
- **Co-execution evaluation**: Delivery Manager scores aliado on execution (1-5 scale)
  - Criteria: Quality of work, customer communication, timeline management, problem-solving
  - Score ≥4/5 = Pass
- **Exam**: Practical scenario exam (2 complex delivery scenarios, write delivery plan for each, >75% eval score)

**Output**: Co-execution score + exam score + Delivery Manager sign-off

---

### Fase 4: MÓDULO 4 - COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE (4h, Semana 5)
**Propietario**: Legal + VP Aliados | **Format**: 1 x 4h session

**Contenido**:
1. Partner policies & governance (1h)
   - Revenue sharing & commission terms
   - Approved use of MetodologIA branding
   - IP ownership (deliverables, templates, customer data)
   - Confidentiality & NDA enforcement
2. Security & compliance requirements (1h)
   - Data protection (GDPR, SOC2, etc) - what aliado must ensure
   - Customer data handling & security standards
   - Incident reporting & escalation
3. Conflict resolution & escalation (1h)
   - When/how to escalate customer issues
   - When/how to escalate inter-partner conflicts
   - Dispute resolution process
4. Q&A + scenario walkthroughs (1h)

**Assessment**:
- **Attendance**: 100%
- **Compliance quiz**: 20 questions on policies, >80% pass (no exceptions; this is legal)

**Output**: Quiz score + Legal/VP Aliados sign-off

---

### Fase 5: CONSOLIDATION & FINAL SIGN-OFF (Semana 6)
**Propietario**: Delivery Manager + VP Aliados

**Final checklist**:
- [ ] Module 1 exam passed (≥70%)
- [ ] Module 2 role-plays passed (2/2)
- [ ] Module 2 exam passed (≥70%)
- [ ] Module 3 co-execution scored ≥4/5 by Delivery Manager
- [ ] Module 3 practical exam passed (≥75%)
- [ ] Module 4 compliance quiz passed (≥80%)
- [ ] Attendance 100% across all modules
- [ ] No unresolved concerns from instructors

**If all pass**: Issue Certificate of Completion

**If any fail**:
- **For exams**: Retake within 2 weeks; if fail again = Remedial + repeat exam
- **For role-plays**: Repeat with coaching; if still fail = Downgrade to "Comisión Pura" (no co-execution)
- **For co-execution score <4/5**: Remedial coaching for 2 weeks, then re-score on different deal
- **For compliance**: Mandatory retake (compliance is non-negotiable)

## 5. Certificación Rúbrica

| Módulo | Assessment | Pass Criteria | Weight |
|--------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Metodología Core | Theory exam | ≥70% | 20% |
| Sales Playbook | 2x Role-plays | Both Pass (2/2) | 20% |
| Sales Playbook | Knowledge exam | ≥70% | 10% |
| Delivery | Co-execution eval | ≥4/5 | 25% |
| Delivery | Practical exam | ≥75% | 15% |
| Compliance | Governance quiz | ≥80% | 10% |

**Final Score**: Weighted average of all assessments
- **≥80%**: Certificate issued
- **70-79%**: Pass with conditions (note areas for improvement)
- **<70%**: Fail → Remedial required

## 6. Casos Borde

### Caso 1: Aliado fails Module 3 co-execution on first try
**Escenario**: Delivery Manager scores aliado 3/5 on co-execution; quality issues
**Resolución**:
- Identify specific gaps (customer management? technical skill? time management?)
- 2-week remedial coaching with same/different deal
- Re-score on new engagement
- If still <4/5: Downgrade to "Comisión Pura" model (no co-execution, higher fees)

### Caso 2: Aliado passes all exams but has personality conflict with Delivery Manager
**Escenario**: Module 3 evaluation is biased due to interpersonal conflict
**Resolución**:
- Escalate to VP Aliados + alternative Delivery Manager
- Re-score Module 3 (co-execution) with neutral party
- Document conflict and lesson-learned

### Caso 3: Aliado team has 50% turnover during certification; original team incomplete
**Escenario**: Certificated team members leave during 6-week program
**Resolución**:
- New team members must retake Modules 1-2 (minimum)
- Skip Module 3 shadow/co-exec if new member; they can apprentice on first deal post-cert
- Original certified members can execute; new members execute under supervision

### Caso 4: Aliado finds error/outdated content in Module 2 playbook
**Escenario**: Case study or market positioning in playbook is wrong
**Resolución**:
- Pause certification for that module
- VP Ventas + Delivery Manager fix content
- Aliado retakes updated module
- Extend timeline but don't compromise integrity

### Caso 5: Aliado refuses to sign compliance agreements before Module 4
**Escenario**: Partner wants to test before signing IP/confidentiality terms
**Resolución**:
- No certification issued until all legal docs signed
- Offer to let aliado review documents before Modules 1-3, but signature required for Module 4
- If aliado refuses: Exit partnership

## 7. Criterios de Aceptación Binarios

| Hito | Evidencia | Owner |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Module 1 Passed | Exam score ≥70% + 100% attendance | Delivery Manager |
| Module 2 Passed | 2/2 role-plays passed + exam ≥70% | VP Ventas |
| Module 3 Passed | Co-exec ≥4/5 + practical exam ≥75% | Delivery Manager |
| Module 4 Passed | Compliance quiz ≥80% | Legal |
| Certificate Issued | All 4 modules passed + final checklist completed | VP Aliados |
| Documentation | Certificate + transcripts stored in partner file | VP Aliados |

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns

### AP1: "Close enough on exam score"
**Anti-pattern**: Passing aliado with 68% on final exam "because they tried hard"
**Prevention**: Strict 70% threshold; retake required if below

### AP2: "Co-execution quality is subjective"
**Anti-pattern**: No rubric for co-exec score; Delivery Manager uses gut feel
**Prevention**: Use 1-5 scale with explicit criteria per level

### AP3: "Certification is check-the-box"
**Anti-pattern**: Issue certificate as formality after modules complete, regardless of performance
**Prevention**: Final checklist + VP Aliados review required before issuing

---

## 9. Fallbacks

### FB1: Aliado fails Module 2 role-play, refuses to repeat
**Fallback**: Document refusal; offer remedial coaching; if still refuse = Conditional Go (sales support from MetodologIA, lower margins)

### FB2: Delivery Manager overloaded; can't supervise co-execution
**Fallback**: Hire external coach (consultant) to supervise co-exec; Delivery Manager spot-checks

### FB3: Aliado needs certificate but is 1 week away from live customer deal
**Fallback**: Issue "Provisional Certificate" if 5/6 modules passed; require final module within 30 days; escalate to CEO

---

## 10. Post-Certification

### Ongoing Quality Assurance
- First 3 deals post-cert: 100% shadowing/review by MetodologIA
- Deals 4-6: 50% spot-check review
- Deals 7+: Risk-based review (high-value, new verticals, escalated issues)
- Annual re-certification: Partner retakes compliance module + delivers 1 full engagement reviewed by Delivery Manager

### Recertification Triggers
- NPS <40 on 2+ consecutive customer surveys
- Major delivery quality issue (scope creep, missed milestones, customer complaint)
- >1 year since last certification: Refresh Module 1 + 4
- Change of key delivery personnel: New person retakes Modules 1-3
