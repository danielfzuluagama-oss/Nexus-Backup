# SOP: Evaluar Fit Aliado
**Versión**: 1.2 | **Última actualización**: 2026-03-24 | **Propietario**: VP Aliados

---

## 1. Propósito

Evaluar sistemáticamente si un potencial aliado tiene alineación estratégica, comercial, operacional y financiera con MetodologIA antes de invertir en onboarding (40-60 horas). Output es una rúbrica binaria: Go (score ≥70%) o No-Go (<70%).

---

## 2. Supuestos Explícitos

1. **Evaluación 4D es suficiente predictor de viabilidad**: La metodología de scoring 4D (Comercial, Operacional, Relacional, Financiera) captura >80% de varianza en likelihood de éxito a 24 meses.

2. **Due diligence requiere múltiples perspectivas**: Fit evaluation NO es solo "VP Aliados opina"; requiere triangulación con VP Ventas (market knowledge) y VP Finanzas (viabilidad de modelo).

3. **Soft signals importan tanto como hard data**: Referencias de mercado, reputación, cultura de partner son predictores tan fuertes como financials o track record.

4. **El threshold de 70% es defensible, no arbitrario**: Históricamente, aliados con score <70% tienen probabilidad >60% de exit en 12 meses; score >70% tiene >65% de reaching >$500K ARR.

5. **Evaluación toma 10-15 horas de investigación**: No es "30-min chat"; requiere research de background, calls con referencias, financialsreview, market analysis.

6. **Documentación es auditable, no opinion**: Rúbrica debe permitir que otra persona re-score con ±5% variabilidad; si variabilidad >10%, criteria están mal definidos.

---

## 3. Ámbito

### Incluido
- Recolección de información de candidato (surveys, calls, documentación)
- Scoring en 4D dimensiones
- Due diligence de referencias (3+ contacts minimum)
- Evaluación de viabilidad de modelo comisión/margen
- Documentación de hallazgos
- Recomendación Go/No-Go

### Excluido
- Negociación de términos (viene después si Go)
- Diseño del contrato (Legal)
- Onboarding (Delivery)
- Marketing/outreach (Marketing)

---

## 4. Flujo y Responsabilidades

### Paso 1: INTAKE (VP Aliados, <1h)
**Input**: Candidato identificado en scouting
**Actividades**:
1. Crear file en Airtable/Salesforce con nombre, contact, vertical, geography
2. Enviar intake form al candidato (5 min completar):
   - Equipo size, años en mercado, clientes actuales
   - Propuesta de valor diferenciante
   - Financials basic (ARR, growth rate si público)
   - Motivación para alianza MetodologIA
3. Schedule call introductorio (30 min) con Aliado CEO/Managing Partner

**Criterio Avance**: Intake form completado + intro call scheduled

---

### Paso 2: INTRO CALL & ASSESSMENT (VP Aliados, 30 min)
**Propósito**: Validar que candidate es serio, entiende MetodologIA, vibe check

**Agenda**:
- Intro de MetodologIA (services, metodología, tipos de partners)
- Background del aliado (historia, team, customers)
- Motivación para partnership (¿por qué ahora? ¿por qué nosotros?)
- Expectativas (ARR target, timeline, modelo preferido)

**Red Flags** (causa immediate No-Go):
- Candidato no ha hecho research en MetodologIA (vague sobre offerings)
- Expectativa de immediate sales (no entiende que requiere ramp-up)
- Competitor directo sin disclosure upfront
- Financial distress signals ("need cash urgently")

**Criterio Avance**: No red flags + mutual interest confirmed

---

### Paso 3: COMMERCIAL ASSESSMENT (VP Aliados + VP Ventas, 2-3h investigation)
**Dimensión**: TAM, go-to-market strategy, sales cycles, competitive landscape

**Investigation checklist**:
- [ ] TAM sizing: Google, Crunchbase research (¿qué % del loro market pueden atacar?)
- [ ] GFY potential: Based on aliado's current customers, ¿cuántos encajan en MetodologIA TAM?
- [ ] Sales cycle: Typical deal size, length of sales cycle, buying process (vs MetodologIA standard)
- [ ] Competition: Who else is aliado's customer buying from? Direct competition to MetodologIA?
- [ ] Reference calls (2x): Call existing customers of aliado; ask about delivery quality, reliability, growth trajectory

**Scoring rubric (0-10 scale)**:
- **9-10**: TAM >$500M, GFY potential >100+ customers, <6mo sales cycle, no direct competition
- **7-8**: TAM >$100M, GFY potential 30-100, <9mo cycle, 1-2 competitors
- **5-6**: TAM $10-100M, GFY potential 10-30, 9-18mo cycle, multiple competitors
- **3-4**: TAM <$10M or GFY <10, >18mo cycle, highly commoditized
- **0-2**: TAM unclear, GFY not viable, or aliado's selling into non-MetodologIA verticals

**Output**: Commercial score (0-10) + written summary of TAM/GFY analysis

---

### Paso 4: OPERATIONAL ASSESSMENT (VP Aliados, 1.5-2h)
**Dimensión**: Infraestructura, capacidad de ejecución, madurez operacional

**Investigation checklist**:
- [ ] Team size & roles: Breakdown of sales/delivery/ops; ¿capacity para co-selling?
- [ ] Processes: ¿Documented sales playbook? ¿Delivery process? ¿CRM/tools?
- [ ] Certifications: ISO, SIL, seguridad data, compliance relevant?
- [ ] Customer base health: NPS, churn, expansion rates (if available)
- [ ] Technology: Stack of tools, integrations, APIs
- [ ] Hiring/Churn: Recent hires/departures? Stability of core team?

**Scoring rubric (0-10)**:
- **9-10**: >20 team, structured processes, ISO/SIL certified, NPS >50, <15% churn
- **7-8**: 10-20 team, documented processes, security practices, NPS 30-50, 15-25% churn
- **5-6**: 5-10 team, ad-hoc processes, basic compliance, NPS 15-30, 25-35% churn
- **3-4**: <5 team, no documented processes, compliance gaps, NPS <15, >35% churn
- **0-2**: Team misalignment, red flags in delivery, reputational issues

**Output**: Operational score (0-10) + assessment of readiness for MetodologIA handoff

---

### Paso 5: RELATIONAL & CULTURAL ASSESSMENT (VP Aliados, 1-1.5h)
**Dimensión**: Leadership, culture, commitment, alignment

**Investigation checklist**:
- [ ] Leadership quality: Founder/CEO background, previous ventures, track record
- [ ] Culture fit: Values, risk tolerance, growth mindset vs short-term gains
- [ ] Commitment: Is this partnership strategic or tactical? Multiple executives engaged or just sales person?
- [ ] Reference calls (2x minimum): Call customers AND ex-employees of aliado; ask about leadership, culture, reliability
- [ ] Advisory fit: ¿Can aliado accept guidance from MetodologIA on go-to-market?
- [ ] Integrity: Any litigation, IP disputes, negative publicity?

**Scoring rubric (0-10)**:
- **9-10**: Founder/CEO >15yr track record, clear culture match, CEO engaged, >3 positive references, no legal/reputational issues
- **7-8**: Leadership >10yr, culture compatible, executive sponsor identified, 2-3 positive references, minor issues resolved
- **5-6**: Leadership mixed, culture questions, single champion, 1-2 references positive but mixed, some history of disputes
- **3-4**: Leadership concerns, culture misalignment, limited commitment, weak references, history of litigation
- **0-2**: Founder/CEO with failed ventures, culture red flags, disengaged, negative references, significant legal/reputational baggage

**Output**: Relational score (0-10) + cultural fit assessment

---

### Paso 6: FINANCIAL VIABILITY ASSESSMENT (VP Finanzas + VP Aliados, 1-1.5h)
**Dimensión**: Model viability, margin/commission, cash flow sustainability

**Investigation checklist**:
- [ ] Current financials: ARR, growth rate, net margin (if provided by aliado)
- [ ] Comisión/margin model: Calculate what % of deal value is available for MetodologIA comisión
  - Typical: Partner buys at 50% discount, sells at 80% of list → 30% margin available
  - Scenario: Partner takes 25%, MetodologIA takes 5% as comisión
- [ ] Break-even timeline: How many deals at assumed conversion rate until partner is NPS positive?
- [ ] Cash flow: Does partner have 6-12mo runway to fund ramp-up before revenue kicks in?
- [ ] ARR target viability: Based on TAM/GFY, ¿realistic to hit >$500K ARR in 24 months?
- [ ] Pricing alignment: Is partner's customer pricing aligned with MetodologIA positioning (premium vs discount)?

**Scoring rubric (0-10)**:
- **9-10**: Clear path to $1M+ ARR in 24mo, 15%+ margin available, strong financials, well-funded
- **7-8**: Path to $500K+ ARR, 12-15% margin, healthy financials, adequate funding
- **5-6**: Path to $300-500K ARR, 10-12% margin, stable but tight financials
- **3-4**: $100-300K ARR potential, 7-10% margin, cash flow concerns
- **0-2**: <$100K ARR potential, <7% margin, funding risk or model not viable

**Output**: Financial score (0-10) + specific ARR projection + comisión model recommendation

|-----------|-----------|--------|-----------------|

| Relacional (Leadership/Culture) | ___ /10 | 25% | ___ |
| Financiera (Model/ARR/Margin) | ___ /10 | 25% | ___ |
| **TOTAL SCORE** | | | **___ /10** |

**Decision Logic**:
- **≥7.0**: Go → Proceed to onboarding
- **6.0-6.9**: Conditional Go → Escalate to VP Ventas + VP Finanzas for joint decision; may require specific conditions met (e.g., "only if aliado hires 2 more sales reps in 90 days")
- **<6.0**: No-Go → Polite decline; offer to re-evaluate in 12 months

## 6. Casos Borde

### Caso 1: Aliado tiene excelente track record pero en diferente vertical que MetodologIA
**Escenario**: Partner with 10yr history, $5M ARR, great culture pero sells only to SMBs (MetodologIA targets mid-market)
**Resolución**:
- Commercial score 3-4 (TAM/GFY mismatch)
- Relational/Operational scores high (7-9)
- Total ~5.5 = No-Go
- PERO: Offer to explore "separate line of business" (SMB offering) with different economics

### Caso 2: Aliado tiene pequeño current ARR pero >80% growth year-over-year
**Escenario**: Partner with $150K ARR, 100% YoY growth, ¿es viable?
**Resolución**:
- Financial score 5-6 (low base, but trajectory strong)
- Scale operational assessment on TEAM trajectory, not just current size
- If team growing 50%+ YoY AND hiring sales/delivery staff → 7/10
- Decision: Conditional Go with milestone: "Must have $500K ARR within 18mo OR partnership pauses"

### Caso 3: Candidato es competitor's employee, wants to start new practice
**Escenario**: 20yr sales leader from Competitor, wants to build practice with MetodologIA
**Resolución**:
- Relational/Commercial high (knows market, has customer relationships)
- Operational low (no team, no processes)
- Financial risk (unproven as business owner)
- Decision: No-Go for "Comisión/Marca Blanca"; but consider Empleado interno if budget exists

### Caso 4: Aliado has strong financials but history of litigation or IP disputes
**Escenario**: $2M ARR, clean team, but has 1-2 lawsuits in background
**Resolución**:
- Financial/Commercial/Operational scores 7-8
- Relational score 4-5 (integrity flag)
- Total ~6-6.5 = Conditional Go
- Condition: Legal review + escrow on future comisión pending litigation resolution

### Caso 5: Fit evaluation suggests Go, but VP Ventas has market intelligence that contradicts
**Escenario**: Rúbrica says 7.2/10, but VP Ventas says "this partner will struggle in this vertical"
**Resolución**:
- Pause final decision; schedule joint meeting (VP Aliados + VP Ventas + VP Finanzas)
- Challenge VP Ventas feedback: specific? Based on which customers? Recent?
- If VP Ventas has credible intel, downgrade Commercial score or escalate to CEO
- Document dissent in the file

## 7. Criterios de Aceptación Binarios

| Criterio | Evidencia | Owner |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| All 4D assessments completed | Scores documented in rúbrica | VP Aliados |
| Reference calls completed (min 2 per dimension) | Call notes summarized | VP Aliados |
| Financial model documented | Spreadsheet with ARR/margin projections | VP Finanzas |
| Rúbrica signed (VP Aliados, VP Ventas, VP Finanzas) | All 3 sign-offs in system | VP Aliados |
| Decision communicated to candidate | Email with rationale (Go/No-Go/Conditional) | VP Aliados |

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns

### AP1: "Strong personal relationship = good fit"
**Anti-pattern**: Scoring aliado high because CEO knows VP Aliados personally
**Prevention**: Enforce independent reference calls; override personal relationship in rubric

### AP2: "One person's assessment is sufficient"
**Anti-pattern**: Only VP Aliados scores; VP Ventas/Finanzas not consulted
**Prevention**: Require 3 signatures minimum; missing = Conditional decision escalated to CEO

### AP3: "Fit changes after onboarding starts"
**Anti-pattern**: Starting onboarding, then discovering during training that fit is poor
**Prevention**: Stick to evaluation criteria; don't onboard "below-threshold" partners

---

## 9. Fallbacks

### FB1: Candidate doesn't provide financial info
**Fallback**: Score Financial dimension as 3/10 and note "unverifiable"; proceed with other dimensions

### FB2: Can't reach references after 3 attempts
**Fallback**: Note as "unable to verify" in rubric; escalate to candidate to provide alternative references

### FB3: VP Ventas/Finanzas not available for final sign-off
**Fallback**: Set 48h deadline for review; if no response = implicit approval; document in file
