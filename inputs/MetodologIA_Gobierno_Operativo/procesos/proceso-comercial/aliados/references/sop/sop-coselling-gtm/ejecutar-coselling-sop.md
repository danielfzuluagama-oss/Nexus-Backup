# SOP: Ejecutar Co-Selling
**Versión**: 1.0 | **Última actualización**: 2026-03-24 | **Propietario**: VP Ventas + VP Aliados

---

## 1. Propósito

Orquestar identificación, desarrollo y cierre de oportunidades de venta conjunta (co-selling) entre MetodologIA y aliados certificados, asegurando alineación comercial, quality standards, y equitable value capture.

---

## 2. Supuestos Explícitos

1. **Co-selling es identificación + joint execution**: No es simplemente "aliado refers deal"; requiere colaboración activa en discovery, proposal, y negociación.

2. **Oportunidades son asignadas, no "first-come"**: Para evitar conflict, ambas parties acuerdan de upfront quién es primary owner de cada opportunity.

3. **Quality gates bloquean malas oportunidades**: No todo what aliado trae es apropiado; pipeline review semanal filtra misaligned deals antes de inversión.

4. **Co-selling aumenta deal size 30-40%** (historically): Joint proposal, shared resources, combined credibility permite explorar larger scope que either party solo.

5. **Commission split es 70-30 default** (MetodologIA-Aliado), adjustable por model (Marca Blanca 50-50, B2B2B negotiated).

6. **Risk de reputación es compartido**: Mala ejecución por aliado afecta MetodologIA brand; requiere SLAs explícitos y quality escala.

---

## 3. Ámbito

### Incluido
- Weekly pipeline reviews (opportunities en co-selling stage)
- Joint discovery & qualification
- Co-authored proposals
- Shared forecasting & pipeline management
- Deal quality gates before execution
- Escalation for disputes/conflicts

### Excluido
- Ongoing customer account management (assignment post-signature)
- Deal-specific negotiation (each party negociates internamente)
- Marketing/co-branding campaigns
- Individual account manager relationships

## 4. Flujo de Co-Selling

### Paso 1: OPPORTUNITY IDENTIFICATION (Semanal)
**Cadencia**: Weekly call (VP Ventas + VP Aliados + Aliado Sales Rep)

**Estructura (30 min)**:
1. **New opportunities from aliado** (10 min)
   - Aliado presents 3-5 warm leads/prospects in pipeline
   - Summary: Company name, size, pain, potential scope, timeline
2. **Qualification review** (10 min)
   - Is opportunity MetodologIA-fit? (Yes/No/Maybe)
   - Does it align with aliado's current capability?
   - Is scope >$250K? (below = not worth co-selling overhead)
3. **Primary owner assignment** (5 min)
   - Who is lead on this opportunity? MetodologIA or Aliado?
   - Shared resources? (e.g., MetodologIA discovery, Aliado implementation)
   - Timeline to next milestone?
4. **Next steps & accountability** (5 min)
   - Who does discovery call? When?
   - Who writes proposal? Timeline?
   - Next pipeline review date + accountability

**Filtering criteria (opportunity must meet ALL)**:
- Potential deal size ≥$250K
- Customer pain aligns with MetodologIA methodology
- Aliado has capability to deliver (or partner with MetodologIA on delivery)
- Timeline is realistic (decision within 6 months)
- No conflicts with existing MetodologIA direct business

**Red flags** (cause opportunity to be marked "No-Go"):
- Customer already in MetodologIA pipeline directly
- Aliado doesn't have capacity to support discovery/RFP
- Scope is below aliado's typical deal size (misaligned positioning)
- Customer is competitor/hostile

**Output**: Updated opportunity list in shared CRM/Airtable

---

### Paso 2: JOINT DISCOVERY (2-3 weeks)
**Propietario**: Primary owner (usually aliado) + supporting party

**Actividades**:
1. **Schedule discovery call** with customer (internal: Aliado + MetodologIA prep call first)
   - Goal: Understand customer outcomes, org structure, decision process, timeline
   - Aliado leads customer relationship; MetodologIA expert supports on methodology fit
2. **Discovery readiness prep** (1 hour joint)
   - MetodologIA provides discovery script/questions (methodology-specific)
   - Aliado adapts to customer context
   - Alignment on desired outcomes from call
3. **Conduct customer discovery call** (1-1.5h)
   - Aliado speaks to business context, pain
   - MetodologIA asks methodology fit, organizational alignment, success metrics
   - Document: Customer outcomes, scope, timeline, stakeholders, decision process
4. **Post-call debrief** (30 min)
   - Go/No-Go decision: Is customer fit strong enough to invest in proposal?
   - Scope estimate: $250-500K? $500K-1M? $1M+?
   - Timeline: 30/60/90 days to decision?
   - Next step: Proposal development or additional discovery?

**Acceptance criteria**:
- Customer pain is clearly articulated
- MetodologIA methodology is directly applicable
- Decision timeline is realistic
- Budget/approval process is understood

**Red flags post-discovery**:
- Customer sees this as "audit/assessment" not transformation
- No executive sponsorship (all conversations with mid-level)
- Timeline >6 months (signals low urgency)
- Scope <$250K (not worth overhead)

**Output**: Discovery notes + Go/No-Go recommendation + scope/timeline estimate

---

### Paso 3: CO-AUTHORED PROPOSAL (2-3 weeks)
**Propietario**: Primary owner drafts; secondary reviews/approves

**Structure**:
1. **Executive summary** (1 page)
   - Business outcomes we'll achieve
   - Why MetodologIA + Aliado are best partners for this
2. **Situation + Opportunity** (1-2 pages)
   - Customer context, challenges, current state
   - Why change is necessary, urgency
3. **Proposed Approach** (2-3 pages)
   - Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3 (waterfall or similar)
   - Roles: What aliado does, what MetodologIA does, what customer does
   - Timeline + milestones
4. **Investment + Value** (1-2 pages)
   - Pricing (breakdown by phase if applicable)
   - ROI calculator / value delivered (in customer's metrics)
   - Payment terms (% upfront, % on milestones)
5. **Team + Experience** (1 page)
   - Bios of key resources (Aliado + MetodologIA)
   - Case studies / relevant experience (anonymized)
6. **Terms & Conditions** (0.5 pages)
   - Scope boundaries
   - Change order process
   - Support/escalation SLAs

**Quality gates before sending**:
- [ ] Both parties align on content (no conflicts in positioning)
- [ ] Pricing is acceptable to both (margin/commission)
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (delivery capacity reviewed)
- [ ] Scope is clear (what's in/out of box)
- [ ] Signature authority identified (who signs for each party)

**Output**: Signed co-proposal + cover email

---

### Paso 4: SHARED FORECASTING (Weekly)
**Cadencia**: Weekly pipeline review call

**Metrics tracked per opportunity**:
- Opportunity name, customer, deal value
- Stage: Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed-Won/Lost
- Probability of close (0-100%)
- Expected close date
- Owner (Primary + Secondary)
- Last update date + next milestone

**Forecast accuracy standards**:
- Opportunities in "Proposal" stage: 60%+ probability minimum
- Opportunities in "Negotiation" stage: 70%+ probability minimum
- Weighted pipeline should be ≥3x of monthly quota (buffer for attrition)

**Weekly actions**:
1. Review all co-selling opportunities by stage
2. Identify blocked deals (stalled >2 weeks without action)
3. Escalate if timeline slipping (needs customer intervention)
4. Celebrate wins, learn from losses

**Output**: Updated forecast in CRM

---

### Paso 5: DEAL QUALITY GATE (Pre-execution)
**Trigger**: Customer signs; deal moves to execution

**Gate checklist** (must pass ALL before handing off to delivery):
- [ ] Contract signed by both customer + MetodologIA + Aliado
- [ ] Scope document attached (what's in/out, phase milestones)
- [ ] SLA documented (response time, escalation, support hours)
- [ ] Aliado is certified in required methodology (Gate G3)
- [ ] Delivery capacity confirmed (does Delivery Manager have bandwidth for support?)
- [ ] Payment terms finalized (invoicing, milestone schedule)
- [ ] Escalation contact identified (customer + Aliado + MetodologIA)

**If gate fails**: Deal goes to "Hold" status until resolved

**Output**: Gate approval + handoff to Delivery Manager

## 5. Casos Borde y Manejo

### Caso 1: Aliado identifies opportunity but wants MetodologIA only in advisory role (low engagement)
**Escenario**: Partner wants to execute alone, keeps 100% of deal value
**Resolución**:
- This is "referral" not "co-selling"; offer 5% finder's fee instead
- If aliado insists on co-selling: Require MetodologIA in delivery (minimum 20% of scope hours)
- Commission split adjusted proportional to value delivery (not just intro)

### Caso 2: MetodologIA and Aliado disagree on customer fit
**Escenario**: Aliado thinks customer is fit; MetodologIA thinks scope is wrong/timing bad
**Resolución**:
- Schedule joint discovery call with customer to validate
- If still disagreement: Escalate to VP Ventas + VP Aliados
- Default: Go with more conservative party (if VP says "No-Go", it's No-Go)
- Document and learn

### Caso 3: Customer requests exclusivity to either MetodologIA or Aliado
**Escenario**: "We only want to work with MetodologIA, not the partner" OR vice versa
**Resolución**:
- For MetodologIA-only: Discuss with customer why aliado adds value; if not critical, allow
- For Aliado-only: Escalate to VP Ventas; may block deal if it violates partnership terms
- Document preference for future similar opportunities

### Caso 4: Aliado's delivery slips mid-project
**Escenario**: Partner delays work; customer satisfaction at risk
**Resolución**:
- VP Aliados calls Aliado leadership immediately (don't wait for weekly standup)
- Root cause analysis: Capacity issue? Quality issue? Communication?
- Option A: MetodologIA takes over remaining work (aliado pays back-charge)
- Option B: Extend timeline with customer consent + corrective plan
- Escalate to contract if recurring

### Caso 5: Opportunity is larger than aliado's capability
**Escenario**: Customer wants $5M scope; aliado's max delivery is $1M
**Resolución**:
- Option A: Co-delivery model (Aliado does Phase 1, MetodologIA does Phases 2-3)
- Option B: Aliado subcontracts delivery to MetodologIA (commission still based on own delivery)
- Option C: MetodologIA takes over 100%; aliado gets finder's fee (5%)
- Agree upfront to avoid conflict

## 6. Criterios de Aceptación Binarios

| Hito | Criterio | Evidencia | Owner |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Opportunity Identified | Meets filtering criteria (≥$250K, fit, timeline) | Logged in CRM with notes | VP Ventas |
| Discovery Completed | Customer outcomes documented, Go/No-Go decided | Discovery notes + recommendation | Primary Owner |
| Proposal Ready | All sections complete, both parties aligned, signed | Co-authored proposal PDF | VP Ventas |
| Forecast Updated | Weekly pipeline review conducted, probabilities realistic | Forecast report | VP Ventas |
| Quality Gate Passed | All contract/SLA/capacity items approved | Gate checklist signed | Delivery Manager |
| Deal Handed Off | Delivery Manager has customer intro + scope doc | Project kickoff scheduled | Delivery Manager |

## 7. Anti-Patterns

### AP1: "Aliado owns relationship; MetodologIA invisible"
**Anti-pattern**: Customer never hears from MetodologIA until execution starts
**Prevention**: MetodologIA in discovery call + joint proposal signature

### AP2: "Opportunity quality is optional"
**Anti-pattern**: Taking <$250K deals for co-selling overhead (not worth it)
**Prevention**: Strict threshold enforcement; referral model for smaller deals

### AP3: "Forecast is fiction"
**Anti-pattern**: Pipeline percentages don't reflect reality; surprises at close
**Prevention**: Weekly reality-check calls; realistic % based on stage

### AP4: "Post-signature conflict"
**Anti-pattern**: Discovering post-contract that customer prefers one party over other
**Prevention**: SLA + escalation contact defined before signature

---

## 8. Fallbacks

### FB1: Weekly pipeline call cancelled multiple times
**Fallback**: Email update of opportunities to stakeholders; escalate to VP Ventas if pattern continues

### FB2: Aliado doesn't follow up on identified opportunity for 3+ weeks
**Fallback**: MetodologIA can take ownership of outreach; adjust commission down if MetodologIA becomes primary

### FB3: Customer signs with wrong SLA terms
**Fallback**: Amendment required before delivery starts; escalate to Legal if customer objects
