# SOP: Auditar Ecosistema de Aliados
**Versión**: 1.0 | **Última actualización**: 2026-03-24 | **Propietario**: VP Aliados + Controller

---

## 1. Propósito

Evaluar semiestralmente la salud, desempeño y viabilidad de cada aliado en el ecosistema MetodologIA, identificando desviaciones temprano e interviniendo para optimizar o exit partnerships de manera ordenada.

---

## 2. Supuestos Explícitos

1. **Auditoría es diagnóstica, no punitiva**: El goal es entender qué está funcionando/no funcionando y arreglarlo, no castigar underperformers.

2. **4D evaluation (Comercial, Operacional, Relacional, Financiero) predice viabilidad**: Mismo framework que evaluar-fit aplica a ongoing audit; aliados con score <60% tienen probabilidad >70% de exit en 12 meses.

3. **Semestrales permite intervention temprana**: Trimestral es over-monitoring; anual es demasiado lento. 6 meses = sufficient data (3-6 deals) + intervention window antes next review.

4. **Metrics son lagging indicators**: NPS baja, ARR trending down, quality issues son síntomas de problemas más profundos (team churn, market shift, capability gap). Audit must investigate root cause.

5. **Renewal es binary decision**: ≥80% all dimensions = auto-renew; <60% = exit negotiation; 60-80% = 90-day improvement plan con clear milestones.

6. **Ecosystem health es finito**: Capacity constraints (Delivery support, VP Ventas time, commercial bandwidth) mean that under-performing aliados should exit para liberar recursos.

---

## 3. Ámbito

### Incluido
- Recolección de metrics por aliado (ARR, pipeline, conversión, NPS, etc)
- 4D scoring (Commercial, Operational, Relational, Financial)
- Root cause analysis para desviaciones
- Stakeholder interviews (customer, aliado leadership, MetodologIA team)
- Renewal recommendation (Auto-Renew / Improvement Plan / Exit)
- Communication a aliado + decision

### Excluido
- Ongoing coaching (that's VP Aliados job post-audit)
- Renegotiation de términos (separate contract process)
- Marketing/brand co-development

---

## 4. Cadencia y Timing

**Audit cycles**:
- **H1 Audit**: May-June (reviews performance Jan-Jun)
- **H2 Audit**: Nov-Dec (reviews performance Jul-Dec)

**Preparación** (3 weeks before audit window):
- Wk 1: Data collection from all systems (CRM, finance, support, etc)
- Wk 2: Root cause analysis for red flags
- Wk 3: Stakeholder interviews + scoring drafts

**Execution** (2 weeks):
- Wk 1: Final scoring + recommendation drafts
- Wk 2: Execution round (communication to aliados)

## 5. Data Collection Phase

### 5.1 Comercial Metrics
**Source**: CRM, Sales forecasting system

**Metrics**:
- **ARR**: Annual recurring revenue generated through aliado (sales + expected renewals)
  - Target vs actual: Is aliado on track to >$500K in 24 months? (yes=green, no=red)
- **Pipeline**: Current qualified pipeline attributed to aliado ($)
  - Health: ≥3x ARR target = healthy
- **Conversion rate**: % of opportunities identified by aliado that close
  - Benchmark: 35-45% (industry-standard for partner channel)
  - Red flag: <25%
- **Deal velocity**: Average time from identification to close
  - Benchmark: 60-90 days (depends on vertical; longer = risk)
- **Referral quality**: % of aliado-referred customers that have retention/expansion
  - Metric: >90% retention, >30% expansion in year 2 (indicates good fit)
- **Geographic/vertical concentration**: Is aliado diversified or single-customer risk?

**Data collection**:
```
For each opportunity attributed to aliado:
  - Customer name, size, vertical, geography
  - Deal value, date closed
  - Customer status (active/churned/expanded)
  - NPS score (if available)
```

---

### 5.2 Operacional Metrics
**Source**: Delivery system, support tickets, project management

**Metrics**:
- **Delivery quality**: NPS of customers delivered by/with aliado
  - Target: >50 (same as MetodologIA direct)
  - Red flag: <40
- **Defect rate**: % of customer complaints, quality issues, rework required
  - Target: <5%
  - Red flag: >10%
- **Milestone adherence**: % of projects that hit timeline milestones
  - Target: >85%
  - Red flag: <70%
- **SLA compliance**: Does aliado meet documented SLAs (response time, escalation)?
  - Target: 95%+ compliance
  - Red flag: <80%
- **Team stability**: Any key departures? Turnover? Growth in team?
  - Red flag: >25% churn in last 6 months
- **Certification status**: Is aliado still certified? Any recertification needed?

**Data collection**:
```
For each customer project with aliado involvement:
  - Delivery phase 1/2/3/4 (kickoff/execution/enablement/handoff)
  - Milestone dates (planned vs actual)
  - Customer NPS (post-delivery survey)
  - Issues/escalations logged
  - Aliado resource hours invested
```

---

### 5.3 Relacional Metrics
**Source**: Executive stakeholder interviews, communication logs

**Metrics**:
- **Engagement level**: Frequency of communication, participation in reviews, strategic alignment
  - Green: Weekly touchpoints, attends reviews, proactive problem-solving
  - Red: <monthly touchpoints, missed reviews, reactive only
- **Conflicts/disputes**: Any unresolved conflicts, contract violations, or relational issues?
  - Green: None or quickly resolved
  - Red: Recurring conflicts, legal involvement
- **Growth mindset**: Is aliado investing in team/capability growth or staying static?
  - Green: Hiring, training, expanding service offerings
  - Red: Flat or declining team
- **MetodologIA knowledge**: Is aliado demonstrating mastery of methodology in customers?
  - Green: Case studies show innovation/adaptation of MetodologIA
  - Red: Cookie-cutter approach, not customizing for customer

**Data collection**:
- Executive stakeholder survey (Aliado CEO, MetodologIA VP Ventas, Account managers)
  - Rating (1-5): Engagement, trustworthiness, problem-solving, growth mindset
  - Open-ended: What's going well? What's biggest challenge?

---

### 5.4 Financiera Metrics
**Source**: Finance system, invoice records

**Metrics**:
- **Margin/Commission paid vs budgeted**:
  - Is aliado earning within expected range? (e.g., 25-30% of deal value)
  - Red flag: Aliado requesting price exceptions / margin pressure
- **Payment compliance**: Does aliado pay MetodologIA on time? (if reseller model)
  - Green: 100% on-time
  - Red: Late payments, disputes
- **Model viability**: Original model assumptions still valid?
  - Revisit: Sales cycle, conversion rate, deal size assumptions
  - If deltas >20%: Escalate for renegotiation discussion
- **ARR contribution to MetodologIA**: What % of total company ARR comes from this aliado?
  - Green: >2% of total (material partner)
  - Red: <0.5% (marginal partner, consider exit)
- **Cash flow sustainability**: Is aliado profitable on partnership, or struggling?
  - Indirect signal: Asking for advance payments, discounts, etc.

**Data collection**:
- Aliado: Quarterly financials (if available/shared)
  - Revenue, margin, team costs, profitability trend
- MetodologIA: Commission paid out, % of ARR

## 6. 4D Scoring Rúbrica

**Scoring template** (Same as Fit Evaluation, adapted for ongoing audit):

| Dimensión | Sub-metric | 0-2 | 3-4 | 5-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 | Score |
|-----------|-----------|-----|-----|-----|-----|------|-------|
| **Comercial** (25%) | ARR vs target | <$100K | $100-250K | $250-500K | $500K-1M | >$1M | ___ |
|  | Pipeline health | <1x target | 1-2x | 2-3x | 3-4x | >4x | ___ |
|  | Conversion rate | <20% | 20-30% | 30-40% | 40-50% | >50% | ___ |
|  | Customer quality (NPS) | <30 | 30-40 | 40-50 | 50-60 | >60 | ___ |
| **Operacional** (25%) | Delivery quality (NPS) | <40 | 40-50 | 50-60 | 60-70 | >70 | ___ |
|  | Defect rate | >15% | 10-15% | 5-10% | 2-5% | <2% | ___ |
|  | Timeline adherence | <70% | 70-80% | 80-85% | 85-95% | >95% | ___ |
|  | SLA compliance | <70% | 70-80% | 80-90% | 90-95% | >95% | ___ |
| **Relacional** (25%) | Engagement/execution | Very low | Low | Medium | High | Very high | ___ |
|  | Conflict resolution | Unresolved | Slow | Moderate | Responsive | Proactive | ___ |
|  | Growth mindset | Stagnant | Minimal | Some investment | Active growth | Aggressive growth | ___ |
|  | MetodologIA mastery | Learning | Basic | Competent | Mastery | Thought leader | ___ |
| **Financiera** (25%) | Margin model viability | Not viable | Marginal | Adequate | Strong | Excellent | ___ |
|  | ARR contribution | <0.5% | 0.5-1% | 1-2% | 2-3% | >3% | ___ |
|  | Payment compliance | Late/disputes | Occasional late | On-time | Proactive | N/A | ___ |
|  | Profitability trend | Declining | Flat | Slightly up | Growing | Strong growth | ___ |

**Final Score Calculation**:
```
Total Score = (Comercial Avg × 0.25) + (Operacional Avg × 0.25) + (Relacional Avg × 0.25) + (Financiera Avg × 0.25)
```

**Scoring scale** (0-10):
- **8.0-10.0**: Excellent (Auto-Renew)
- **7.0-7.9**: Strong (Auto-Renew with condition)
- **6.0-6.9**: Adequate (Improvement Plan required)
- **5.0-5.9**: Concerning (Improvement Plan + close monitoring)
- **<5.0**: Critical (Exit discussion)

## 7. Root Cause Analysis

For any red flag score (dimension <6.0), conduct root cause analysis:

**Investigation template**:
1. **What's the gap?** (e.g., ARR $200K vs $500K target)
2. **Why?** (potential causes)
   - Market: TAM smaller than expected? Competition?
   - Capability: Aliado can't sell/deliver MetodologIA effectively?
   - Execution: Poor pipeline management, lost deals?
   - Alignment: Aliado prioritizing other partners?
3. **Evidence**: Customer feedback, aliado interviews, metrics trends
4. **Corrective action**: What needs to happen to improve?
   - Scenario A: Coaching/capability building (6-12 weeks)
   - Scenario B: Market shift accepted; reset targets
   - Scenario C: Exit (partner not viable)

---

## 8. Stakeholder Interviews

**Interview aliado leadership** (30-45 min):
- How is partnership going from your perspective?
- What's working well? What's challenging?
- Are you on track to ARR targets? Why/why not?
- Any capability gaps or resources needed?
- Long-term strategy: Do you want to continue growing with MetodologIA?

**Interview MetodologIA account team** (for aliado customers):
- How is customer satisfaction? NPS trend?
- Any quality issues? Escalations?
- Is aliado collaborative, responsive?
- Would you recommend expanding this partnership?

**Interview customer references** (2-3 customers delivered by/with aliado):
- How was delivery experience? NPS?
- Would you recommend aliado to others?
- Any concerns or issues?

## 9. Renewal Decision

### Decision Matrix

| Score | Recommendation | Action |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| ≥8.0 | Auto-Renew | Extend contract for another 12 months; no changes |
| 7.0-7.9 | Renew with conditions | Extend contract; document 1-2 specific KPI improvements required in next review |
| 6.0-6.9 | Conditional Renewal | Issue 90-day improvement plan; specific milestones; reassess at end of 90 days |
| 5.0-5.9 | Improvement Plan + Monitoring | 90-day plan; weekly check-ins; escalation if not improving |
| <5.0 | Exit Discussion | Schedule formal conversation; develop transition/exit plan; 30-90 day wind-down |

### Improvement Plan (for scores 6.0-6.9)

**Template**:
1. **Current state**: What's the gap? (e.g., "ARR at $250K, target $500K")
2. **Root cause**: Why? (e.g., "Limited sales capacity, poor lead qualification")
3. **Milestones** (90 days):
   - Milestone 1 (30 days): Hire 1 FTE sales rep
   - Milestone 2 (60 days): 10+ qualified pipeline opportunities
   - Milestone 3 (90 days): $100K closed (on track to $500K ARR by 24mo)
4. **Support from MetodologIA**: Co-selling support? Training? Other resources?
5. **Re-eval criteria**: How will we know if improvement plan is working?

**Checkpoints**: Weekly check-in (VP Aliados + Aliado leadership); 30/60/90 day assessments

## 10. Casos Borde

### Caso 1: Aliado has strong commercial metrics but poor operational quality
**Escenario**: $800K ARR, high pipeline, but customer NPS = 35
**Resolución**:
- Comercial: 8/10 (strong)
- Operacional: 4/10 (poor)
- Total ~6.0 = Conditional Renewal
- Improvement plan: Quality audit, retraining, 1 supervised customer project

### Caso 2: Aliado scores high but is pivot'ing away from MetodologIA vertical
**Escenario**: Partner success, but CEO says "we're now focusing on AI products, not consulting"
**Resolución**:
- Relacional: 3/10 (misaligned)
- Whole assessment: ~5.0 = Exit discussion
- Option: Accept pivot, move to "dormant" status with option to reactivate

### Caso 3: External market shock (recession) impacts aliado ARR
**Escenario**: Aliado was on track but customer base downsizing; ARR dropped 30% in 6 months
**Resolución**:
- Acknowledge market headwind; don't penalize for external factors
- Adjust ARR expectations for this cycle (reset target to $350K)
- Monitor for recovery; if still down in H2, discuss exit

### Caso 4: Aliado flags compliance/legal issue mid-audit
**Escenario**: Partner discloses that a customer discovery revealed ethical/legal concern
**Resolución**:
- Immediate legal review
- Pause renewal decision pending investigation
- If serious: Escalate to CEO; may trigger early exit

## 12. Anti-Patterns

### AP1: "Audit is blaming session"
**Anti-pattern**: Aliado sees audit as punishment, not helpful diagnostic
**Prevention**: Lead with "How can we help you succeed?" not "Why are you failing?"

### AP2: "Metrics are cherry-picked"
**Anti-pattern**: Only showing favorable metrics to justify renewal
**Prevention**: Use complete 4D framework; transparent about red flags

### AP3: "No follow-up after audit"
**Anti-pattern**: Issuing improvement plan but not checking progress
**Prevention**: Weekly check-ins; document progress; escalate if slipping
