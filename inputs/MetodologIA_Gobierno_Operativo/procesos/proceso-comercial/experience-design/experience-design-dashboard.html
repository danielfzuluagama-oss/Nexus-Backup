Decision Log (Decisiones Criticas del Modelo)

Decisiones arquitectonicas clave del blueprint y su status de reversibilidad:

Decisiones ACTIVAS (Vigentes)
ADR-001
6 fases vs 4 fases originales (F0 Awareness + F5 Advocacy)
Active Risk: Low ● ● ● ● ●
Expansion to 6 phases provides better customer segmentation and clearer journey mapping. Reversibility: High (can revert to 4-phase if needed).
ADR-002
Separate B2C y B2B SOPs completamente (no overlap)
Active Risk: Medium ● ● ● ●
Different personas require different coaching styles. Reduces operational complexity. Reversibility: Medium (would need template refactoring).
ADR-003
P1 (Profesional) como segmento prioritario (40% resources)
Active Risk: High ● ● ●
Highest LTV and referral potential. Reversibility: Low (requires full resource reallocation if changed).
ADR-004
F3 Practica as bottleneck phase (max 10 clientes/coach)
Active Risk: Medium ● ● ●
Quality over scale during delivery. Maintains NPS > 70. Reversibility: Medium (impacts hiring and capacity planning).
ADR-005
MOT-3 "Aha!" es metric canonica de CJ health
Active Risk: Low ● ● ● ●
Single measure of journey success. Correlates with NPS, referrals, and long-term retention. Reversibility: High.
Decisiones DEPRECATED (Historicas)
ADR-000
4-phase original modelo (Diagnostico→Autonomia) — DEPRECATED 2026-03
Deprecated Risk: Low ● ●
Replaced by 6-phase model. Archived but still referenced in v1 documentation.
