# Proceso: Entregar Bootcamp de Transformación

**Versión:** 2.0 | **Vigencia:** 2026-01-2026-12 | **Propietario:** Dirección de Operaciones | **Última Actualización:** 2026-03-24

---

## Certificación de Moat

![Moat Certified](https://img.shields.io/badge/MOAT-Certified-success) Proceso de transformación acelerada, medible (NPS ≥50, completion ≥95%, job placement ≥60% B2C) y replicable con 4 gates de decisión binarios.

---

## Vitamin (Visión)

> **Un bootcamp no es currículo; es transformación comprimida.** Equipos/individuos salen con frameworks internalizados, proyectos VIVOS en marcha, confianza en su capacidad. No enseñamos teoría; construimos bajo presión (3 semanas), aprendiendo-haciendo.

---

## Visión Estratégica

Bootcamp es rampa de **mastery acelerada** vs. workshop (awareness) o curso (episódico). Genera:

- **Skill mastery**: Práctica 60h, feedback real-time, ≥80% assessment pass
- **Project artifacts**: MVP/framework/automation entregado (no simulación)
- **Squad cohesion**: Aprendizaje en equipo = relaciones durables + peer support post-bootcamp
- **Behavioral anchoring**: Nuevos ritmos, hábitos, patterns (no one-off learning)
- **Alumni economy**: Cohort → community permanente (job board, mensual webinars, referrals)

---

## Supuestos Explícitos

Este proceso asume:
1. **Facilitador Lead asignado**: Experiencia ≥1 bootcamp previo + pedagogía de project-based learning certificada
2. **Cohort tamaño 8-20**: <8 = dinámicas pobres; >20 = imposible mentoría individualizada
3. **Proyecto scope realista**: 3 semanas = MVP (no product pulido). Si scope >MVP → renegociar T-14
4. **Squad composition balanceada**: Facilitador forma squads (no autoselección) para evitar skill concentration
5. **Tech infrastructure confiable**: Slack, Notion, Zoom, LMS accesibles sin outages. Si outage >2h → pause bootcamp

---

## Límites (Qué NO cubre)

- Colocación laboral (dominio: Alumni)
- Programas de mentoría 1:1 post-bootcamp (separate product)
- Implementación en producción del capstone (client responsibility)
- Funding/venture preparation (beyond skill transfer)

---

## Handoff Points

**Entrada**: Bootcamp confirmado + cohort formada (8+ personas) → FASE 1 gatillo
**Salida**: Capstone presentations completadas + NPS ≥50 + certificates issued → Alumni Manager relevo (job board, networking)

## FASE 1: DISEÑO CURRICULAR (T-21 a T-7)

### Objetivo
Personalizar curriculum para contexto específico, definir proyecto capstone, entrenar facilitadores, setup technical infrastructure.

### Duración
**14 días** | Esfuerzo: 40 horas (Especialista Curriculum, Facilitador Lead, Tech Ops)

### Responsables Principales
- **Especialista Curriculum**: Diseño, customización, contenido
- **Facilitador Lead**: Dirección pedagógica, facilitator training
- **Tech Ops**: Plataforma de learning, automation
- **Account Manager**: Alineación con cliente, definición de proyecto

### Inputs Requeridos
1. Bootcamp type (BC-01 a BC-05)
2. Cohort profile: roles, industry, current skills, pain points
3. Project brief: challenge específico a resolver en 3 semanas
4. Facilitator team assigned
5. Learning platform infrastructure available

### Outputs Esperados
1. Customized curriculum (week-by-week, session-by-session)
2. Capstone project rubric + success criteria
3. Facilitator playbook (lesson plans, discussion guides)
4. Tech platform configured (Slack, Notion, Zoom, LMS)
5. Pre-work assignments + baseline assessments
6. Cohort handbook (schedule, expectations, community guidelines)

### SOPs Referenciadas
- **SOP-DISEÑO-CURRICULAR**: Design curriculum (detailed pedagogy)
- **SOP-TECH-SETUP**: Setup learning platform

### Decisiones Gating

| Decisión | Criterio | Responsable | Escalación |
|----------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Curriculum Gate** | ✓ Customized curriculum approved ✓ Project brief defined ✓ Facilitators trained ✓ Platform ready | Especialista Curriculum | CEO Delivery si issues |
| **Scope Risk** | Project es muy complejo para 3 semanas o desaligned con bootcamp type | Account Mgr + Curriculum | Simplify scope o extend timeline |

## FASE 2: ONBOARDING COHORTE (T-7 a T-0)

### Objetivo
Forma cohort psychology, establece norms, preps participants, crea sense of community ANTES de que empiece el bootcamp.

### Duración
**7 días** | Esfuerzo: 20 horas (Facilitador Lead, Ops)

### Responsables Principales
- **Facilitador Lead**: Community building, kick-off event
- **Coordinador Ops**: Logistics, communications
- **Tech Ops**: Platform access, onboarding flows

### Actividades

**T-7: Cohort Formation + Pre-work**
- Asignar squads (3-4 personas por squad, diverse mix)
- Enviar welcome email con:
  - Squad assignments
  - Pre-work assignment (self-paced, 5 hours)
  - Slack workspace link
  - Platform access (Notion, Zoom, etc.)
  - Community guidelines + expectations
  - Schedule (all 21 days, with flexibility noted)

**T-5: Pre-work Completion Deadline**
- Participants complete baseline assessment
- Facilitador monitors completion (target ≥ 90%)
- Send reminder to stragglers

**T-3: Kick-off Event (In-person or Zoom)**
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Purpose: Build energy, clarify expectations, create belonging
- Agenda:
  - Welcome + vision (30 min): Why are we here, what are we building
  - Squad introductions (30 min): Tell us your story, your goal
  - Capstone project overview (30 min): Here's what you're solving
  - Expectations + norms (20 min): How we work together
  - Team photo (if in-person)
  - Breakout: Squad bonding (1:1 coffee chats)

**T-2: Final Logistics**
- Confirm participant access to all platforms
- Share schedule (exact times, facilitators, links)
- Send "you're ready!" email with pep talk

### Inputs Requeridos
1. Squad assignments (balanced by skills, backgrounds)
2. Pre-work materials + baseline assessment
3. Capstone project brief
4. Platform access configured
5. Kick-off event logistics confirmed

### Outputs Esperados
1. Cohort roster + squad assignments documented
2. Pre-work completion rate ≥ 90%
3. Baseline assessment data (skills snapshot at T-0)
4. Kick-off event recording + photos
5. Community established (Slack active, introductions done)
6. Participant handbook distributed

### KPIs de Onboarding

| KPI | Target | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Pre-work completion** | ≥ 90% | Facilitador |
| **Platform adoption** | ≥ 80% active on Slack/Notion by T-0 | Tech Ops |
| **Kick-off attendance** | ≥ 95% of cohort | Ops |
| **Squad formation quality** | No conflicts, balanced skills | Facilitador |

### Decisiones Gating

| Decisión | Criterio | Responsable |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Cohort Readiness Gate** | ✓ ≥ 95% attended kick-off ✓ ≥ 90% completed pre-work ✓ Squads formed ✓ All access confirmed | Facilitador Lead |

## FASE 3: FACILITACIÓN SEMANAL (T-0 a T+15)

### Objetivo
Facilitar 3 semanas intensivas de aprendizaje, proyecto-based learning, con cadencia semanal clara y entregas medibles.

### Duración
**21 días (3 semanas completas)** | Esfuerzo: 60 horas facilitación + 20 horas async support

### Responsables Principales
- **Facilitador Principal**: Dirección, enseñanza, mentoría
- **Facilitador Asistente**: Squad support, office hours, tech
- **Coordinador Ops**: Cadencia, comunicaciones, registro

### Estructura Semanal Repetible

**Lunes: Kick-off + Context**
- Duration: 90 min (todos)
- Facilitador presenta:
  - Learning objectives para la semana
  - Connection a previous week
  - Project milestone due this week
- Breakout sessions por squad (15 min): Sprint planning
- Q&A + clarity

**Martes-Thursday: Skill-building Sessions + Squad Work**
- 60 min skills session (todos):
  - Concept deep-dive, live demo, examples
  - Q&A real-time
- 90 min squad work (unsupervised):
  - Aplicar concepto a su proyecto
  - Asistente disponible (office hours)
  - Slack para async questions

**Friday: Showcase + Retro**
- 60 min squad showcases:
  - Cada squad presenta progreso (5 min per)
  - Feedback de otros squads
  - Facilitador comments
- 30 min cohort retrospective:
  - What went well?
  - What was hard?
  - Weekend prep (optional learning)

### Week-by-Week Breakdown

**WEEK 1: Foundations + First Momentum**
- Mon: Intro to core framework + project scoping
- Tue-Thu: 3 skill modules (asynch practice between)
- Fri: Squad showcases first project iteration
- **Deliverable**: Validated scope document (1 per squad)

**WEEK 2: Advanced Concepts + Squad Depth**
- Mon: Connect week 1 to deeper application
- Tue-Thu: 3 advanced modules (real project integration)
- Fri: Squad showcases progress toward MVP
- **Deliverable**: Working prototype or artifact (per squad)

**WEEK 3: Mastery + Capstone Prep**
- Mon: Synthesis of week 1-2, capstone presentation prep
- Tue-Wed: Final project sprints (minimal teaching)
- Thu: Presentation rehearsal with facilitador feedback
- Fri: Capstone presentations (audience: peers + external guests)
- **Deliverable**: Polished capstone deck + artifact

### Session Framework (60-min skill module)

| Time | Activity | Facilitator Role | Participant Role |
|------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| 0-5 min | Context & Why | Establish relevance | Listen, connect to project |
| 5-25 min | Concept + Demo | Teach, show live, narrate | Take notes, ask clarifying Qs |
| 25-40 min | Guided Practice | Guide activity, circulate | Do hands-on, experiment |
| 40-55 min | Application | Challenge to their project | Apply to own context, iterate |
| 55-60 min | Wrap + Assign | Clarify homework, Q&A | Note next steps |

### Async Learning Infrastructure

- **Notion database**: Lessons, resources, templates (searchable)
- **Recording library**: All sessions recorded + transcribed
- **Slack channels**:
  - `#general`: Announcements
  - `#squad-[name]`: Squad-specific
  - `#office-hours`: Facilitator availability (Tue-Thu 4-5pm)
  - `#wins`: Celebrate progress
  - `#help`: Technical issues
- **Loom/video**: Quick tips, walkthroughs (5-10 min videos)

### Squad Dynamics Management

**Squad Size**: 3-4 people (optimal for psychological safety + accountability)

**Squad Composition**: Mix by:
- Skills (1 strong + 1-2 developing + 1 intermediate ideally)
- Backgrounds (different roles, companies if B2C)
- Personality (not all quiet, not all dominant)

**Squad Rituals**:
- **Daily standups** (async or 15-min sync):
  - What did I do yesterday?
  - What will I do today?
  - What's blocking me?
- **Mid-week check-in** (30 min): How are we progressing toward Friday milestone?
- **Friday showcase**: Present to cohort

**Conflict Resolution**:
- Facilitador monitors Slack for tension
- Private 1:1 if needed
- Team reset conversation if necessary
- Last resort: rebalance squad (week 2 only)

### Inputs de Facilitación
1. Customized curriculum + lesson plans
2. Project briefs + rubrics
3. Tech platform (Slack, Notion, Zoom, LMS)
4. Facilitator playbook + discussion guides
5. Capstone rubric + exemplars

### Outputs de Facilitación
1. Weekly attendance + engagement logs
2. Squad project artifacts (weekly milestone submissions)
3. Recording library (all sync sessions)
4. Facilitator notes on participant progress
5. Formative assessment data (quizzes, reviews)
6. Capstone project artifacts (polished)

### KPIs de Facilitación

| KPI | Target | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Session attendance** | ≥ 90% on Monday/Friday, ≥ 70% async engagement | Ops |
| **Project quality trajectory** | Week 1: Rough → Week 2: Functional → Week 3: Polished | Facilitador |
| **Participant satisfaction** | Weekly pulse check: ≥ 4/5 | Ops |
| **Skill mastery** | Formative assessments ≥ 70% | Facilitador |
| **Squad cohesion** | Retro feedback ≥ 4/5 on team dynamic | Facilitador |

### Decisiones Gating

| Señal | Acción | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| **Attendance <70%** (mid-week) | 1:1 check-in, understand blocker | Facilitador |
| **Project complexity too high** | Scope reduction (negotiate with squad) | Facilitador Lead |
| **Squad conflict** | Team dynamics discussion or squad rebalance | Facilitador + Ops |
| **Participant struggling** (formative <60%) | Mentoring sessions, pair with strong peer | Facilitador Asistente |

## FASE 4: GRADUACIÓN & ALUMNI (T+16 a T+30)

### Objetivo
Celebrar logros, medir impacto, activar alumni network, conectar con oportunidades (jobs, projects, community).

### Duración
**15 días** | Esfuerzo: 16 horas (Facilitador Lead, Ops, Alumni Manager)

### Actividades

**T+16-18: Capstone Presentations (Live Event)**
- Duration: 3-4 hours (ideally in-person)
- Format:
  - Intro (10 min): CEO/Founder welcome
  - Squad presentations (8-10 min each, 4-5 squads): Problem, solution, results
  - Q&A + feedback (5 min per squad)
  - Awards/recognition (best project, most improved, etc.)
  - Alumni ceremony + certificate
  - Networking reception (food, drinks, vibe)
- Attendance: Cohort + mentors + external guests (investors, industry leaders, press if applicable)
- Recording: Capture for portfolio/social

**T+19: Skills Assessment & Certificates**
- Administer final assessment (quiz or project review)
- Generate certificates:
  - Skills achieved (with rubric proof)
  - Project completion proof
  - Participant signature + facilitador signature
  - Shareable digital + printable
- Send certificates individually + batch social media post

**T+20: Alumni Survey + Impact Data**
- Administer survey:
  - What was most valuable?
  - Will you use these skills in next 30 days?
  - Would you recommend to others? (NPS)
  - Interest in next program?
  - Interest in job placement / ongoing mentoring?
- Calculate bootcamp NPS (target ≥ 50)
- Publish highlights (video quotes, testimonials)

**T+21-30: Alumni Activation**
- Create alumni Slack channel (permanent community)
- Monthly alumni webinar (office hours, skill-building, speaker)
- Job board (curate opportunities for alumni)
- Cohort reunion (virtual happy hour in month 2)
- Alumni referral program (reward for bringing next cohort)

### Inputs Requeridos
1. All capstone projects ready
2. Final assessment rubrics
3. Certificate templates
4. Alumni survey
5. External guests for presentations (optional)

### Outputs Esperados
1. Capstone presentation recording + photos
2. Certificates (digital + print-ready)
3. Bootcamp impact report:
   - NPS score
   - Skills mastery (assessment data)
   - Project quality scores
   - Testimonials
   - Lessons learned
4. Alumni network established (Slack, email list)
5. Job placement tracker (% employed in field within 90 days)

### KPIs de Graduación

| KPI | Target | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **Capstone project quality** | ≥ 80% score on rubric | Facilitador |
| **NPS (Bootcamp)** | ≥ 50 | Ops |
| **Skills mastery** | ≥ 80% pass final assessment | Facilitador |
| **Certificate delivery** | 100% within T+3 of graduation | Ops |

| **Job/project placement** (90 days) | ≥ 60% in relevant role/project | Alumni Mgr |

## Segmentación: Variantes de Ejecución

### Variante 1: Corporativo B2B (In-Company)

**Contexto**: Bootcamp for team within single organization.

**Adaptaciones**:
- **Curriculum**: 80% customized to company tech stack, processes, projects
- **Project**: Real company problem/initiative (not simulated)
- **Facilitation**: On-site or hybrid (more in-person days)
- **Timeline**: Can compress to 2 weeks if high intensity
- **Outcomes**: Direct ROI (process improvement, new capability, shipped feature)
- **Alumni**: Team stays together post-bootcamp (project continuation)
- **Pricing**: 18M COP (standard)

**Key Consideration**: Higher customization cost, but higher ROI perception

---

### Variante 2: Abierto B2C (Open Enrollment)

**Contexto**: Public bootcamp; participants from different companies/backgrounds.

**Adaptaciones**:
- **Curriculum**: Framework standard; examples span industries
- **Project**: Simulated but realistic (provided scenario, data, constraints)
- **Facilitation**: Fully virtual (async-first, with 2-3 sync sessions per week)
- **Timeline**: Fixed 3-week schedule (no compression)
- **Outcomes**: Skill acquisition + portfolio project (for job seeking or freelancing)
- **Alumni**: Cohort forms community (ongoing support, job board, referrals)
- **Pricing**: 800K COP individual (or cohort discounts)

**Key Consideration**: Larger cohorts (15-20) increase viability; strong async structure critical

---

### Variante 3: Híbrida (Empresa + Individuals)

**Contexto**: Corporate team + external participants learning together.

**Adaptaciones**:
- **Curriculum**: 50% customized (mix of company-specific + general frameworks)
- **Projects**: Parallel tracks (company project for corporate team, simulated for individuals)
- **Facilitation**: Hybrid sync sessions (both in-person + Zoom)
- **Squad composition**: Mix corporate + individuals (knowledge transfer value)
- **Alumni**: Separate alumni networks (corporate stays internal, individuals join public network)

## Casos Borde (Decisiones Críticas)

**Caso 1: Cohort dropout rate >30% (>6 personas en week 2)**
Trigger: Más de 30% abandona/missed >2 sesiones sin aviso
Decision: Root cause investigation inmediata (1:1 calls):
- Work crisis? (proyecto deadline del client) → negotiate flexibility
- Engagement (no ven value)? → pair con mentor, refocus
- Tech blocker? → immediate support
- Personal? → pause + restart option (defer, no charge)
If still dropping after intervention: Escalate to CEO Delivery (pause bootcamp vs. continue at reduced quality)

**Caso 2: Squad conflict → psicología rota (week 2, tension level 3+)**
Trigger: Squad miembro pide rebalance, o facilitador ve Slack tension alta
Decision: Immediate action:
- Facilitador 1:1 con cada persona (separate) - escuchar
- Facilitated team conversation - coach hacia resolution
- If no improvement: Move uno a otro squad (week 2 only, window cierre)
- If breakdown: Escalate to CEO + Coaching partner

**Caso 3: Project scope = impossible en 3 semanas (mid-week 2)**
Trigger: Squad realiza "nunca vamos a completar esto"
Decision: Real talk conversation:
- Reducir scope a MVP (defer "nice-to-haves")
- O pivotear a demo mode (present concept, no full product)
- Or extend timeline (bootcamp becomes 4 weeks)
Facilitador + squad decide juntos. Principle: Protect learning + confidence, sacrifice scope if needed.

**Caso 4: Facilitador principal no disponible week 3**
Trigger: Enfermedad, emergency personal
Decision: Backup facilitador toma relevo (con asistente sênior como support)
- Brief express (4h intensivo)
- Shadow week 2 finales (handoff clarity)
- Proceder week 3 con nuevo facilitador
Escalate to CEO if no backup disponible (no deferral opción = cohort completition risk)

**Caso 5: Tech stack falla >4h (week 1, Slack o Notion DOWN)**
Trigger: Platform outage
Decision: Immediate fallback:
- Zoom → Google Meet (ok)
- Slack → Email lists + Google Docs (slower, but viable)
- Notion → Google Sheets/Docs (less elegant, functional)
- Resume when restored
Escalate to IT if vendor issue. Documentar para infrastructure review.

**Caso 6: Assessment data muestra <70% mastering key skill (week 2 formative)**
Trigger: Quiz muestra que >30% no entienden concept X
Decision: Reteach session:
- Reschedule one skill session (comprimir otro)
- Simpler explanation, concrete example
- 1:1 office hours para struggling participants
- Don't proceed to advanced concepts until foundation solid

## Decisiones de Diseño con Justificación

**D1: ¿Por qué 3 semanas (no 2, no 4)?**
- 2 semanas: Insufficient time for behavioral anchoring (skills fade post-bootcamp)
- 3 semanas: Óptimo = introducción + profundización + masterpiece (capstone)
- 4 semanas: Attrition rate rises (life happens, commitments shift)
- Data interna: Completion rate 95% @ 3w, 85% @ 4w, 60% @ 2w

**D2: ¿Por qué squad size 3-4 (no 2, no 5)?**
- 2 personas: Falta de diversidad, peer learning limitado
- 3-4: Sweet spot (accountability + diversity + manageability)
- 5+: Alguien queda invisible, equipo demasiado grande

**D3: ¿Por qué NPS ≥50 target bootcamp (vs. ≥45 workshop)?**
- Bootcamp es 20x más intenso que workshop → expectativa más alta
- Bootcamp cuesta más → satisfacción debe ser proporcional
- 50 es "muy bueno"; <45 indica structural issue

**D4: ¿Por qué 60%+ job placement target (B2C bootcamp)?**
- Bootcamp es credencial laboral (no workshop)
- 60% = realista (algunos no buscan job, algunos tienen gaps personales)
- <45% = bootcamp educational effectiveness cuestionable

## Anti-Patterns (QUÉ NO HACER)

- NO permitir autoselectión de squads (leads to homogenous skill groups, poor learning)
- NO prometer job placement con garant ía (riesgo legal + demanda imposible)
- NO completar capstone por squad (derrota propósito learning)
- NO tolerar squad sin standups (sin accountability = chaos)
- NO cambiar facilitador sin transición formal (handoff clarity crítico)

## Protocolos de Emergencia

### Emergencia 1: Facilitador Principal No Disponible (Week 1)

**Protocol**:
1. Immediately activate backup facilitador
2. Briefing express (facilita handbook + lesson plans)
3. Announce cohort: "We have a facilitador change; here's why [positive framing], here's who [intro]"
4. Assign Asistente to shadow week 1 (handoff clarity)
5. Document: root cause analysis

---

### Emergencia 2: Cohort Dropout >30%

**Triggers**: More than 6 people drop out (or miss >2 sessions without explanation)

**Protocol**:
1. Facilitador identifies root cause (1:1 interviews)
2. Common causes:
   - Work crisis (project deadline)
   - Personal (family emergency)
   - Engagement (not feeling value)
   - Tech (platform/access issues)
3. Targeted intervention:
   - Work crisis: Extend timeline, negotiate flexibility
   - Personal: Pause + restart later option
   - Engagement: Pair with mentor, refocus on project relevance
   - Tech: Immediate support, alternative setup
4. If still dropping: Escalate to CEO Delivery (consider pause/restart)

---

### Emergencia 3: Project Scope Too Ambitious

**Signals**: Week 2 midway, squads realize can't complete capstone by Week 3

**Protocol**:
1. Facilitador convenes squads: "Real talk: is scope realistic?"
2. Options:
   - Reduce scope (MVP only, defer nice-to-haves)
   - Extend timeline (bootcamp becomes 4 weeks)
   - Pivot to demo mode (present concept, not full product)
3. Facilitador + squad collaboratively decide
4. Reset expectations: "We're optimizing for learning + delivery, not perfection"

---

### Emergencia 4: Tech Platform Failure (Zoom, Slack, Notion)

**Protocol**:
1. If <1 hour downtime: Async work (recorded lesson, Slack tasks)
2. If 1-4 hours: Reschedule to later that day or next day
3. If >4 hours: Alternative platform:
   - Zoom → Google Meet
   - Slack → Email + Notion
   - Notion → Google Docs
4. Communicate: "Here's the workaround"
5. Document: Report to IT, review infrastructure

---

### Emergencia 5: Interpersonal Conflict in Squad

**Protocol**:
1. Facilitador Asistente monitors Slack tensions early
2. **Tension level 1**: Facilitador adds levity, reframes conflict as creative friction
3. **Tension level 2**: Facilitador has 1:1 with each person (understand perspectives)
4. **Tension level 3**: Team facilitated conversation (3 people + facilitador)
   - Each person shares: perspective, concern, what they need
   - Facilitador coaches toward resolution
5. **Tension level 4**: Rebalance squad (move 1 person to another squad, if time)
6. **Last resort**: Escalate to CEO Delivery
