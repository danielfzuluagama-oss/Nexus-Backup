- [ ] Capstone presentations (memorable, celebratory, recorded)

## T+16 to T+18: Capstone Presentations (Live Event)

### Pre-Event Prep (T+15)

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead + Coordinador Ops (4 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Schedule rehearsal** (T+14, 2 hours):
   - Each squad does dry-run (8-10 min)
   - Facilitador gives feedback: clarity, pacing, energy
   - Squads adjust (emphasize problem, be specific about solution)

2. **Prepare logistics** (T+15, 2 hours):
   - Venue confirmation (in-person preferred):
     - Room setup (stage, chairs for audience, sound system)
     - Projector/screen for demos
     - Lighting (especially if recordings)
     - WiFi stress-tested
     - Catering (snacks, drinks, reception after)
   - Or: Zoom setup if virtual
     - Breakout rooms for squad presentations
     - Host controls (mute, spotlight)
     - Recording verified

3. **Invite external guests** (T+14-T+15, 1 hour):
   - Target: 5-10 external guests (if possible)
     - Mentors/sponsors
     - Investors (if startup bootcamp)
     - Industry experts
     - Journalists (optional)
   - Email: "[Bootcamp name] Capstone Presentations [Date/Time] [Zoom/Address]"
   - Subject: "Celebrate our cohort's achievements"

4. **Prepare materials** (T+15, 1 hour):
   - Print ceremony program (all squad names, project titles)
   - Certificates (print if not digital)
   - Props: medals, trophies (if awards given)
   - Music playlist (low-key background)

### Event Day (T+16 or T+17 or T+18)

**Duration**: 3-4 hours

**Agenda**:

| Time | Activity | Duration | Facilitador Role |
|------|----------|----------|-----------------|
| 0:00-0:15 | Reception + settling | 15 min | Greet guests, settle vibe |
| 0:15-0:25 | Welcome + opening remarks | 10 min | CEO or Facilitador: set stage |
| 0:25-1:40 | Squad presentations | 75 min | Emcee: introduce each squad, manage Q&A (5-10 min per) |
| 1:40-1:55 | Awards/recognition | 15 min | Celebrate: best project, most improved, audience choice |
| 1:55-2:05 | Certificates | 10 min | Celebrate individuals (quick personal note for each) |
| 2:05-2:30 | Keynote (optional) | 25 min | External speaker (mentor, alumni, industry) |
| 2:30-3:00 | Reception + networking | 30 min | Informal, food, photos |

**Presentation Format per Squad**:

| Segment | Time | Guidance |
|---------|------|----------|
| **Problem statement** | 2 min | "We set out to solve..." Clear, specific |
| **Solution demo** | 5 min | Live or recorded demo (have backup) |
| **Results/learning** | 2 min | What did you learn? What would you do differently? |
| **Q&A** | 1 min | Audience questions (1-2 max) |

**Facilitador as emcee**:
- Introduce each squad warmly
- Call out something specific about their project
- Facilitate Q&A (reframe harsh questions, celebrate good questions)
- Manage time (gently cut off if overrunning)
- Model enthusiasm + respect

**Awards** (optional, fun):
- **Best Project**: Most complete, innovative, user-tested
- **Most Improved**: Biggest growth from Week 1 to Week 3
- **Audience Choice**: Voted by attendees
- **Crowd Favorite**: Most energy, best presentation

**Certificates**:
- Print or digital (both OK)
- Include: Participant name, bootcamp name, date, skills demonstrated, facilitador signature
- Shareable (encourage LinkedIn posts)

**Post-event**:
- Take group photo (all cohort + facilitator team)
- Collect testimonial quotes (video or written)
- Upload videos to Notion (within 24 hours)
- Post to Slack #wins

**Output**: Capstone event completed, presentations recorded, photos captured, testimonials gathered

## T+19 to T+20: Final Assessment + Certificate Delivery

### Skills Assessment

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead (2 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Administer final assessment** (if not already done):
   - Format: Quiz or project review
   - Measure: Can they demonstrate mastery of learning objectives?
   - Scoring: Pass/Fail or rubric score (0-10)
   - Example question: "Apply [concept] to solve [new problem]"

2. **Review assessment results**:
   - Calculate: % who scored ≥ 80% (target ≥ 85%)
   - Identify: Anyone below threshold (mark for additional support)
   - Synthesize: Overall skill lift from baseline to final

3. **Document findings**:
   - Spreadsheet: [Name | Baseline Score | Final Score | Delta | Proficiency Level]
   - Share with Ops for reporting

### Certificate Generation & Delivery

**Responsable**: Coordinador Ops (2 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Create certificates**:
   - Template: [Company logo] Certificate of Completion
     ```
     This certifies that [NAME]
     has successfully completed
     [BOOTCAMP NAME]
     [DATES]
     
     Skills demonstrated:
     - [Skill 1]
     - [Skill 2]
     - [Skill 3]
     
     [Facilitador signature] [Date]
     ```
   - Format: PDF + PNG (for social media)
   - Personalize with names

2. **Send certificates**:
   - Email to each participant:
     - Attachment: PDF certificate
     - Link to LinkedIn sharing template
     - Prompt: "Share on LinkedIn! Here's a suggested post..."

3. **Track delivery**:
   - Mark in CRM: "Certificate issued"
   - Note date delivered
   - Track social media posts (who shared on LinkedIn)

**Output**: Certificates generated + delivered, skills assessment complete

## T+20 to T+22: Impact Survey + NPS

### Bootcamp Impact Survey

**Responsable**: Ops + Specialista Nurturing (1 hour setup; 1 week collection)

**Survey Content** (Typeform, 3-5 min):

```
BOOTCAMP IMPACT SURVEY

1. On scale 0-10, how likely are you to recommend this bootcamp?
   (NPS question)

2. What was most valuable about the bootcamp?
   (Open-ended)

3. What could be improved?
   (Open-ended)

4. In the next 30 days, will you use what you learned?
   (Yes / Somewhat / No)

5. Are you interested in:
   ☐ Next bootcamp (advanced)
   ☐ 1:1 coaching
   ☐ Job board / placement support
   ☐ Alumni community
   (Check all that apply)

6. Your role at company:
   (Dropdown)

7. Industry:
   (Dropdown)
```

**Send survey** (T+20):
- Email with Typeform link
- Deadline: T+24 (4 days to respond)
- Reminder: T+22 (2 days before)

**Target**: ≥ 70% response rate

### NPS Analysis

**Responsable**: Ops + Especialista Contenido (1.5 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Calculate NPS**:
   - Count promoters (9-10)
   - Count detractors (0-6)
   - NPS = [(Promoters - Detractors) / Total] × 100
   - Target: ≥ 50

2. **Segment results**:
   - NPS by role (manager vs. IC)
   - NPS by bootcamp type (if multiple cohorts)
   - NPS by engagement (high attendee vs. low)

3. **Analyze qualitative feedback**:
   - What's mentioned as most valuable?
   - What's the most common improvement?
   - Patterns in verbatim feedback?

4. **Create summary report** (1 page):
   - NPS score + breakdown
   - Top 3 positive feedback themes
   - Top 2 improvement areas
   - Testimonial quotes (best ones highlighted)

**Output**: NPS analysis complete, testimonials identified

## Casos Borde (Graduación)

**Caso 1: Squad capstone project not ready T+15 (incomplete, buggy)**
Trigger: Demo prep muestra que proyecto está rough
Decision: Options:
- Pivot to "demo mode" (concept + early-stage prototype, no full product)
- Request short extension (T+17 vs. T+16) - only if acceptable to client
- Facilitate honest presentation ("We built X, learned Y, here's roadmap")
Principle: Learning > perfection. Honest delivery > faked completion.

**Caso 2: NPS <45 (below bootcamp target)**
Trigger: Survey results muestra NPS <45, detractors >30%
Decision: Root cause analysis ASAP (T+20):
- Facilitador self-eval: ¿facilitation issue? pedagogía?
- Curriculum review: ¿content gaps? ¿pacing off?
- Logistics/tech review: ¿infrastructure blockers?
Action: Document findings, create improvement plan para próximo cohort.
Escalate to CEO if systemic issue.

**Caso 3: External guest cancels day-before capstone event**
Trigger: Mentor/speaker cancels T+15
Decision: Immediate:
- Ops finds replacement (otro facilitador, mentor, alumni)
- Or simplify agenda (skip keynote, extend networking)
- Proceed as-is; guest es nice-to-have, not critical
Principle: Event happens with or without guest.

**Caso 4: Video recording fails (capstone not captured)**
Trigger: Tech issue, presentations happened but no recording
Decision: Fallback:
- Facilitador + Asistente create "transcript" document
- Ops collects participant-submitted videos (ask squad to share phone recordings)
- Create photo gallery + testimonial quotes
Rationale: Transcript + photos preserve memory, just less polished.

**Caso 5: Participant requests certificate typo fix day-before ceremony**
Trigger: "My name is spelled wrong"
Decision: Fix immediately (15 min)
- Regenerate certificate
- Print new copy for ceremony
- Send digital version same day
Escalate if more than 1 error (data entry process issue)

**Caso 6: Alumni engagement drops week 2 post-bootcamp (only 20% active Slack)**
Trigger: Alumni channel crickets, low engagement
Decision: Alumni Manager intervenes:
- Personal outreach (not broadcast): "What would make community valuable?"
- Identify 3-4 "champions" to drive content
- Launch job board officially (if not live)
- Schedule first webinar (4 weeks post)
Principle: Community needs active cultivation, not passive waiting.

**Caso 7: Participant expressa doubt about next bootcamp interest, but was promoter (NPS 9)**
Trigger: "I loved this, but next bootcamp is expensive for me"
Decision: Account Manager conversation:
- Acknowledge value ("Glad you got value")
- Explore options (payment plan? scholarship? wait for discount?)
- No pressure; plant seed for future
Escalate to Commercial if strategic account.

## Anti-Patterns (QUÉ NO HACER)

- NO sacrifice capstone presentation quality for time (rehearsal is critical)
- NO ignore NPS <45 data (investigate root cause)
- NO treat alumni activation as "someone else's job" (Facilitador + Alumni Manager own it together)
- NO promise job placements directly (curate board, candidates choose)
- NO delay certificates (trust = on-time delivery)
