# SOP: Onboarding Cohorte

**Versión:** 2.0 | **Vigencia:** 2026-01 | **Dueño:** Facilitador Lead | **Última Actualización:** 2026-03-24

---

## Identificación

- **Código SOP**: SOP-OC-BC
- **Proceso Padre**: Entregar Bootcamp (Ver: entregar-bootcamp-proceso.md)
- **Fase**: Onboarding Cohorte (T-7 a T-0, 7 días intensivos)
- **Responsable Principal**: Facilitador Lead (community builder, psychologically-safe culture setter)
- **Responsable Secundario**: Coordinador Ops (logistics, tech access, communications)
- **Stakeholders**: Tech Ops (platform), Squad participants, Account Manager (client)
- **Input desde**: SOP-DC-BC (curriculum + project brief)
- **Output hacia**: SOP-FS-BC (Facilitación Semanal)
- **Gate**: Cohort Readiness (T-0) - ≥95% kick-off attendance, ≥90% pre-work completion

---

## Objetivo Binario

Convertir grupo de individuos dispersos en COHORT COHESIVO con:
- [ ] Squads formados (3-4 personas, balanced skills + personalities)
- [ ] Psychological safety established (safe to struggle, ask questions)
- [ ] Pre-work completed ≥90%
- [ ] Platform access 100% funcional
- [ ] Kick-off event ≥95% asistencia
- [ ] Momentum alto hacia Week 1

---

## Supuestos & Límites

**Supuestos**:
1. Roster validada T-7 (nombres, roles, emails activos, experience levels)
2. Curriculum finalizado T-7 (project brief, learning objectives claros)
3. Slack workspace pre-created, Notion database ready
4. Facilitador Lead ha participado en ≥1 bootcamp anterior

**Límites (No cubierto)**:
- Resolución de tech issues profundas (IT support domain)
- Coaching pre-bootcamp (out of scope)
- Pre-onboarding for prerequisite knowledge (assumimos ya lo saben o facilitador lo cover in Week 1)

## Proceso Paso-a-Paso (T-7 a T-0)

### T-7: Cohort Formation + Welcome Package

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead + Coordinador Ops (4 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Form squads** (1.5 hours):
   - Analyze cohort profile (roles, skills, personalities, backgrounds)
   - Create 3-4 squads of 3-4 people each (balanced):
     - Mix skills (not all strong, not all weak)
     - Mix personality types (not all introverts, not all extroverts)
     - Mix backgrounds/companies (if B2C)
   - Assign squad names (fun names create identity)
   - Document assignments (sheet with names, squads, emails)

2. **Create welcome email** (1 hour):
   - Subject: "You're In! Welcome to [Bootcamp Name] 🚀"
   - Content:
     ```
     Hi [Name],

     Welcome to the cohort! We're thrilled to have you here.

     HERE'S YOUR SQUAD
     [Squad name]: [Names + roles]
     Your squad lead: [Name] (the one with most experience)

     WHAT'S NEXT
     1. Join Slack workspace [link] (accept invite today)
     2. Complete pre-work [link to assignment] by [T-5]
     3. Attend kick-off event [date/time/link] (T-3)

     PRE-WORK: 5 hours, self-paced
     - Baseline assessment (what do you know today?)
     - Intro module (get your mind ready)
     - Optional: Tool setup/familiarization

     EXPECTATIONS
     - Show up (sync sessions Mon/Fri at [time])
     - Engage (ask questions, share ideas)
     - Support squad (we succeed together)
     - Build something real (capstone project matters)

     Questions? Reply to this email.

     See you soon,
     [Facilitador Lead]
     ```
   - Attach: Squad assignments, calendar (all 3 weeks), pre-work link

3. **Send welcome package** (1 hour):
   - Individual welcome emails (personalized with squad)
   - Batch email to all (squad assignments, calendar, community guidelines)
   - Create Slack workspace if not already
   - Add all participants to Slack

4. **Document baseline** (0.5 hours):
   - Create roster with: name, email, squad, role, prior experience
   - Share with facilitator team
   - Set expectations: track attendance, engagement, progress

**Output**: Welcome emails sent, squads assigned, roster created

---

### T-6 to T-5: Pre-work Assignments + Baseline Assessment

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead + Tech Ops (2 hours daily monitoring)

**Pasos**:

1. **Pre-work design** (async, self-paced, 5 hours total):
   - Module 1: Bootcamp mindset (30 min video)
     - What is this bootcamp?
     - Why it matters
     - What success looks like
   - Module 2: Baseline assessment (1.5 hours):
     - Skills quiz (where are you today?)
     - Self-reflection (goals, challenges, commitments)
   - Module 3: Tool familiarization (2 hours):
     - If technical bootcamp: get environment set up
     - If not: familiarity with learning platform
   - Module 4: Optional deep-dive (1.5 hours):
     - Reading or video on bootcamp topic
     - Get curious, not comprehensive

2. **Monitor completion** (T-5 evening):
   - Check who completed pre-work (target ≥ 90%)
   - For non-completers: friendly nudge email or Slack DM
     - "Hey [name], we haven't seen your pre-work yet. All good? Need help?"
     - Offer: "We can sync on [time] if you're stuck"

3. **Analyze baseline assessment data**:
   - Skills distribution (who's strong, who needs support?)
   - Identify low-skill participants (mark for extra mentoring)
   - Look for engagement patterns (who's excited, who's uncertain?)
   - Document findings (for facilitator team)

**Output**: ≥90% pre-work completion, baseline skills data, facilitator debrief

---

### T-4 to T-3: Community Building + Kick-off Prep

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead + Coordinador Ops (6 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Activate Slack community** (1 hour):
   - Post welcome message to #general (set tone: enthusiastic, supportive)
   - Intro posts from facilitator team (who we are, what we care about)
   - Squad intro threads (squads introduce themselves to each other)
   - Encourage 1:1 Slack intros between squad members

2. **Plan kick-off event** (2 hours):
   - Logistics: Date/time, venue (in-person preferred) or Zoom link
   - Agenda:
     - 0-30 min: Welcome, ice breaker, bootcamp vision
     - 30-60 min: Participant intros (squad by squad, 2 min per squad)
     - 60-90 min: Capstone project overview + examples
     - 90-120 min: Expectations, norms, team photo, breakout bonding
   - Materials: Slides, ice-breaker activity, team photo backdrop (if in-person)

3. **Send kick-off reminder** (1 hour):
   - T-4: Calendar invite (if not already sent)
   - T-3: Email reminder (logistics, what to bring, what to expect)
   - Optional: short video of facilitator (personal touch, builds connection)

4. **Prepare squad breakout sessions** (2 hours):
   - Post squad-specific guide to Slack (squad leads get copy):
     ```
     SQUAD INTRO BREAKOUT (30 min)

     1. Intros (10 min): Name, role, company, 1 fun fact
     2. Goals (10 min): Why you're here, what you want to build
     3. Commitment (10 min): How you'll show up, what you need from squad

     Squad lead: Take notes, share back with team after
     ```

**Output**: Kick-off event ready, community activated on Slack, squad breakout guides prepared

---

### T-2: Final Logistics + Platform Verification

**Responsable**: Coordinador Ops + Tech Ops (2 hours)

**Pasos**:

1. **Verify platform access** (1 hour):
   - Check all participants have:
     - [ ] Slack workspace access
     - [ ] Notion/Google Drive access
     - [ ] Zoom link for Monday sync
     - [ ] LMS/materials access
   - For anyone missing access: Fix immediately, send confirmation

2. **Confirm participant info** (30 min):
   - Send confirmation email with all logistics:
     ```
     BOOTCAMP STARTS TOMORROW!

     ✓ Kick-off event: [Date/Time] [Zoom link or address]
     ✓ First session: [Monday, exact time] [Zoom link]
     ✓ Schedule: [Link to full calendar]
     ✓ Platform access: [Links to Slack, Notion, etc.]
     ✓ Squad: [Squad name + members]

     Come as you are. Bring curiosity. Leave anxiety at the door.

     See you in 24 hours!
     ```

3. **Facilitator team final prep** (30 min):
   - Facilitador Lead + Asistente(s) sync (15 min):
     - Review final roster
     - Discuss any known challenges or needs
     - Confirm roles/responsibilities
   - Tech test (15 min):
     - Zoom call test (audio, video, screen share)
     - Slack + Notion access verify
     - Recording setup test
     - Backup plan if tech fails

**Output**: Platform access confirmed, tech tested, team aligned

---

### T-0: Kick-off Event (Live)

**Responsable**: Facilitador Lead (2-3 hours in-person/sync)

**Session Agenda**:

| Time | Activity | Facilitator | Participant Role |
|------|----------|------------|------------------|
| 0:00-0:05 | Welcome | Principal | Settle, attention |
| 0:05-0:15 | Bootcamp vision | Principal | Listen, absorb why |
| 0:15-0:25 | Ice breaker | Principal | Share, laugh, connect |
| 0:25-1:00 | Squad intros | Facilitator per squad | Introduce self, share goals |
| 1:00-1:30 | Capstone overview | Principal | Understand the project |
| 1:30-1:45 | Expectations + norms | Principal | Agree to commitments |
| 1:45-2:00 | Team photo + breakout | Asistente | Build squad bonds |

**Facilitator Talking Points**:
- "This is not a course; it's a co-creation"
- "You'll build something real in 3 weeks"
- "Your squad is your support system"
- "Failure is learning; we celebrate it"

**Expected Outcomes**:
- Participants feel welcomed + excited
- Squads have bonded (at least initial connection)
- Project is understood
- Psychological safety established (safe to struggle, ask questions)

**Post-event**:
- Take photos (with permission)
- Post group photo to Slack (#wins)
- Send recording to Slack (for those who missed)
- Squad leads post reflection to Slack

**Output**: Kick-off event completed, community activated, Week 1 momentum established

## Casos Borde (Onboarding)

**Caso 1: Participante "ghost" (inscrito, no responde a comunicaciones T-5)**
Trigger: Welcome email enviado, sin respuesta, pre-work no iniciado
Decision: T-5: Follow-up call (Account Manager o Facilitador Lead)
- "Hi [name], we haven't seen you yet. Everything OK?"
- Si no responde T-4: Check in con Account Manager/client contact ("Is [person] still coming?")
- Si no confirmed T-2: Marca como "tentative", proceed sin asumir presencia
Principle: Don't wait; surface issues early

**Caso 2: Squad formed, pero uno es "outsider" (lenguaje, background, skills muy diferente)**
Trigger: Facilitador nota en kick-off que uno no encaja con su squad
Decision: Monitor Week 1, si tension visible:
- Option A: Mentoring pairing (mentor + outsider, para aceleración)
- Option B: Rebalance to another squad (week 1 only window)
- Option C: 1:1 coaching para onboarding acelerado
Principle: Inclusión es importante, pero squad cohesion también

**Caso 3: Tech platform access falla para >2 personas T-2**
Trigger: Slack bot no invitó, Notion permissions wrong, etc.
Decision: IT triage inmediata (sameday)
- Ops coordinator troubleshoots en paralelo
- If <2h fix: proceed como planeado
- If >2h fix: manual onboarding (video walk-through alternative)
Escalate: Si sigue broken T-0: notify Facilitador Lead

**Caso 4: Pre-work completion baja (<70% por T-5)**
Trigger: Menos del 70% completó pre-work assignment
Decision: T-5: Nudge email/message
- No-judgment reminder
- Offer async extension (hasta T-6 23:59)
- Offer tech support si es blocker
- Facilitador prepared para Week 1 without assuming pre-work done (reteach if needed)

**Caso 5: Kick-off event attendance drops to 80% (4 de 20 missing T-0)**
Trigger: Algunos no pueden asistir a última hora
Decision: Proceed con 16 (80% is acceptable)
- Facilitador sends recording + kick-off notes a no-shows
- Personal follow-up: "Sorry you missed it. Here's what you need to know..."
- Not dealbreaker, pero documentar reason (work, tech, personal)

---

## Anti-Patterns (QUÉ NO HACER)

- NO auto-select squads (permite clustering de skill/personality; Facilitador debe balancear)
- NO ignorar participante "ghost" pre-kick-off (escalate temprano)
- NO permitir participante optar out of kick-off ("I'll read notes later") - asistencia non-negotiable
- NO sobre-personalize pre-work (complexity frena completitud; keep simple)
- NO perder roster durante onboarding (single source of truth en shared sheet)

---

## Fallbacks Operativos

| Falla | Fallback | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Tech access fail >2 personas | Manual onboarding (video guide + personal support) | Tech Ops + Ops |
| Pre-work completion <70% | Extend deadline T-6, reteach in Week 1 if needed | Facilitador Lead |
| Kick-off attendance 80% | Proceed, send recording + notes to no-shows | Facilitador Lead |
| Squad formation incomplete T-7 | Facilitador forms manually (no esperes autoselección) | Facilitador Lead |
| Participants "ghosting" pre-T-0 | Account Manager 1:1 call ASAP | Account Manager |
