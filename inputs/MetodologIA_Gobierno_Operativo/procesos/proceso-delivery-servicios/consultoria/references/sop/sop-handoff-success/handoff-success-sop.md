# SOP: Handoff & Success - Transición a Éxito Sostenible
**Versión:** 1.1 (Elevado 10x) | **Fecha:** 2026-03-24 | **Certif. Moat:** SOP-CON-HANDOFF-2401

---

## OBJETIVO

Transición ordenada de delivery (consultor-led) a success (cliente autonomous). Preservar momentum, activar recompra + referral loops, establecer bases para sostenibilidad 6-12 meses.

---

## SUPUESTOS EXPLÍCITOS

1. **Supuesto: Proyecto alcanzó ≥80% métricas objetivo.** Si <80% → no "success", es "at-risk". Protocol diferente (ver Caso Borde 1)
2. **Supuesto: Owner roles son claros & quieren ownership.** Si personas reluctantes → adoption post-handoff falla. Validar compromiso.
3. **Supuesto: Documentación existe & es accesible.** Si documentación incompleta → handoff incompleto. Require completitud antes ceremony
4. **Supuesto: Budget post-proyecto (soporte) fue discussed.** Si "sorpresa" en Handoff que soporte cuesta → mala experiencia → no recompra

---

## LÍMITES DEL PROCESO

**Handoff NO cubre:**
- Garantía sostenibilidad indefinida: Solo 3 meses post-handoff. Después = cliente responsabilidad
- Scaling sin nosotros: Si cliente quiere escalar a 10 áreas, necesita Evolución (no handoff support)
- Nuevas iniciativas: Si cliente pide "mientras estamos aquí, implementemos [X]", es fuera de scope (sugerir Tracción 2)

**Trigger & Entrada**

**Trigger:**
- Tracción completada con ≥80% métricas pasando
- Evolución completada con adoption ≥70% + internal consultants trained

**Entrada Requerida:**
- ✓ KPI dashboard (live, tracked, comparativo before/after)
- ✓ Documentación completa (playbooks, FAQs, troubleshooting)
- ✓ Owner roles formally assigned (RACI final confirmado)
- ✓ Support model chosen (Opción 1/2/3)
- ✓ Training completado (super-users, managers, IT certificados)

---

## CASOS BORDE & DECISIONES DE DISEÑO

### Caso Borde 1: Proyecto "At-Risk" (<80% métricas pasando)

**Trigger:** Post-Tracción/Evolución, KPIs están en 65% target

**Acción (decisión binaria):**
- Opción A: **Success + Extended Support** — Proclamar success, ofrecer Light Touch ($200k/trim) por 6 meses para recuperar gap
- Opción B: **Redesign Sprint** — 2-semana intensive rescue (no extra charge) para traer to 80%+
- Opción C: **Transparent "Partial Success"** — Honesto: "Alcanzamos 65%. Aquí están aprendizajes. Próximos pasos: [X]"

**Recomendación:** Opción A o C (honestidad). Opción B solo si gap es "simple fix" (no systemic)

**Communication:** No ocultar. Cliente prefiere honestidad + soporte continuing que "spin" success falso

---

### Caso Borde 2: Owner Reluctant (asignado pero "no quiere")

**Trigger:** Handoff ceremony, appointed owner dice "no estoy cómodo"

**Acción (48h):**
- Diagnosis: ¿Falta confianza? Miedo? O simplemente "no me interesa"?
- Si confianza: +1 sesión coaching + mentor assigned (Light Touch, pago)
- Si miedo legítimo: Re-assign a persona más confident
- Si "no interesa": Honest conversation — mejor encontrar genuine owner now que ghost owner later

**Risk:** Reluctant owner = project dies in 2 meses post-handoff

---

### Caso Borde 3: Presupuesto soporte "shock"

**Trigger:** QBR Handoff, CSM mentions "$200k/trim" soporte light touch. Cliente: "¿Qué? Pensé que estaba included"

**Acción:**
- Referencia propuesta original: "Aquí está explícito — support es separate, 3 opciones"
- Si cliente expected included: Error comercial anterior. Opción: Regalar 1 mes soporte (goodwill) + clarify futuro
- Si cliente budget-constrained: Offer Opción 1 (full autonomy, $0) + mention recompra opportunity Sem 12

**Prevention:** Support model debe stated claramente in kick-off + emailed weekly

---

### Decisión: 3 meses post-support vs 6 vs indefinida

**Elegimos 3 meses porque:**
- Adoption stabilizes Sem 8-12 (post-project). After Sem 12, adoption trajectory is clear
- Si cliente needs soporte post-3 meses, es sign transformación didn't stick (cultura fallo, ownership fallo)
- Longer support = dependency, no autonomy. 3 meses = sweet spot accountability + support

**Trade-off:** Algunos clientes "fall off cliff" Sem 16. Pero better that than 12 meses support que crea dependency. Cliente debe own su cambio

## FASE 1: DOCUMENTACIÓN COMPLETA (Último Sprint de Proyecto)

### Documentación de Entrega

**1. Knowledge Base:**
- Procesos nuevos (playbooks, ½-1 página c/uno)
- Decision frameworks
- Troubleshooting guides
- FAQ (10-15 preguntas comunes)
- Video tutorials (3-5 min c/uno)
- Links a recursos externos

**2. Technical Documentation (si aplica):**
- Architecture diagram (sistemas, integraciones)
- Configuration guide (cómo setear si nueva herramienta)
- API documentation (si hay integración custom)
- Disaster recovery / backup procedures

**3. Governance & Ownership:**
- "Who is responsible for what" (matriz RACI)
- Escalation procedures (quién contactar si [problema X])
- Steering committee charter (si aplica)
- Decision rights (quién decide qué, cuándo)

**4. Change Management Legacy:**
- Change log (qué cambió, cuándo, por qué)
- Lessons learned (qué salió bien, qué aprender para próximo)
- Success stories (testimonios de usuarios)
- Resistance overcome (cómo manejamos resistencia, aprendizajes)

### Delivery Ceremony (Último Día de Proyecto)

**Format:** 90 min, all stakeholders + team MetodologIA

**Structure:**
- 0-15 min: Celebration of impact (metrics, testimonials)
- 15-30 min: "You are now in charge" (transfer of responsibility)
- 30-60 min: Documentation walkthrough (cómo usar qué, dónde encontrar)
- 60-75 min: Support model explained (quién contactar post-proyecto)
- 75-90 min: Q&A + closing

**Output:**
- Video grabado
- Certificate of Completion (cliente recibe)
- Thank you email + contact info para follow-up

## FASE 2: OWNERSHIP TRANSFER (Última Semana)

### Responsability Handoff

**Clear Ownership by Role:**
- Process Owner: [Name], accountable por adopción & eficiencia
- Technical Owner: [Name], accountable por infrastructure & tools
- Change Champion: [Name], accountable por comunicación & support
- Executive Sponsor: [Name], accountable por sostenibilidad & recursos

**Accountability Confirmation:**
- Email oficial de cliente: Confirmación de responsabilidades
- Meeting con nuevo "owners": Primer plan, primeros 90 días

### Transition to In-House Support

**Before Handoff:**
- Super-users + champions entrenados
- Knowledge base completamente poblada
- Support tickets process establecido (cómo reportar issues)
- Escalation paths definidos

**Support Model Options:**

**Opción 1: Full Autonomy ($0)**
- Cliente es 100% responsible por sostenibilidad
- MetodologIA available para emergencies (pago por hora, $100k COP/h)
- Suitable para: Clientes con fuerte capacidad interna, cambio fue simple

**Opción 2: Light Touch ($200k/trimestre)**
- Monthly check-in con consultor (1h): "Cómo va? Dónde dolor?"
- Ad-hoc support vía email/Slack: Preguntas, troubleshooting
- Quarterly business review (2h): Metrics, next steps
- Suitable para: Transformación mediana, algunos champions

**Opción 3: Active Support ($500k/trimestre)**
- Weekly office hours (1h): Coaching, Q&A
- Bi-weekly check-ins (1h): Coaching de champions
- Monthly strategic review (2h): "Cómo escalamos?"
- Refactoring support: Ajustes pequeños si needed
- Suitable para: Transformación profunda (Evolución), scalabilidad crítica

## FASE 3: POST-IMPLEMENTATION MONITORING (3 MESES)

### Month 1: "Honeymoon Phase"

**Owner Actions:**
- Daily support (via Slack/email): Answer questions, troubleshoot
- Weekly check-in calls (1h): "How's adoption? Where's friction?"
- Dashboard watching: Track KPIs daily, alert if trending wrong
- Champion coaching: 1:1s to support change leaders

**Consultant Role (if Light/Active support):**
- Available for escalations
- Coaching calls with champions
- Dashboard analysis (is adoption on track?)

**Metrics to Watch:**
- Adoption rate: Are people using new process? (target: ≥50% by day 30)
- System usage: Logs showing activity (or radio silence = problem)
- Error rates: New process causing issues?
- User sentiment: Frustration or enthusiasm?

### Month 2: "Settling In"

**Owner Actions:**
- Shift from daily to every-other-day support
- Focus on adoption laggards: Who's not using it? Why?
- Refinement: Small fixes based on user feedback
- Communication: Share wins, celebrate adoption

**Consultant Role (if Light/Active support):**
- Weekly check-in (vs daily)
- Coaching champions on how to handle resistance
- Potential pivot: If adoption stalling, escalation + intervention

**Metrics:**
- Adoption: Target ≥70% by day 60
- KPIs: Are metrics moving in right direction?
- Support tickets: What are 3 most common issues?

### Month 3: "Sustainable?"

**30-Day Gate Decision:**

**QBR (Quarterly Business Review, 90-min formal):**

**Attendees:** Sponsor, process owners, champions, consultant (if applicable)

**Agenda:**
- Metrics review (vs baseline, vs targets)
  - What's progressing well?
  - What's lagging?
  - Why?
- Adoption review
  - Who's using it? Who's not?
  - Resistance / friction points
- System health
  - Bugs, issues, technical problems
  - Performance (speed, reliability)
- Next 90 days
  - Refinements needed
  - Escalation / deepening
  - Recompra opportunity?

**Decision Gates:**

| Scenario | Decision | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Metrics ≥80%, Adoption ≥70%** | ✅ SUCCESS | Transition to minimal support / celebrate |
| **Metrics 50-79%, Adoption 50-69%** | ⚠️ ON TRACK | Continue support, coaching champions, refinements |
| **Metrics <50%, Adoption <50%** | 🔴 AT RISK | Escalation: Deeper intervention, possible root cause analysis |

## FASE 4: RECOMPRA & REFERRAL ACTIVATION

### Recompra Loop (Month 1-3)

**Timeline & Triggers:**

**Week 1-2 (Post Delivery):**
- Customer Success Manager (CSM) assigned
- Welcome call (30 min): Intro, support options, expectations
- Email: "Here's your support path forward"

**Month 1:**
- CSM: Light touch check-in (email)
- Commercial: Hold back (let success settle)

**Month 2-3 (Post QBR):**
- CSM: Identify recompra opportunities
  - "We fixed [area 1], now [area 2] could use similar treatment"
  - "Your team mastered [process], ready to scale to [other area]?"
  - "We measured $XX value. Ready to invest in next initiative?"
- Commercial: Intro call if recompra fit
  - Propose Tracción (next quick win) or Evolución (deep transform)
  - Timeline, investment, expected ROI

**Conversion Target:** ≥30% of Tracción clients → Evolución upgrade
**Conversion Target:** ≥20% of Evolución clients → next Tracción or ongoing support

### Referral Activation (Month 2-6)

**Ideal Moment:** After QBR success, when client is evangelizing

**Outreach:**
- CSM: "You've transformed [area]. Would you referer someone with similar challenge?"
- Email: "Here's a referral link / how to introduce us"
- Incentive: Crédito de horas o descuento en próximo proyecto

**Referral Process:**
- Client refers peer
- Commercial: Discovery call
- If fit: Propuesta Diagnóstico/Tracción
- If close: Credit or discount applied

**Conversion Target:** ≥40% of clients give ≥1 referral

---

## FASE 5: LONG-TERM SUCCESS (6-12 MONTHS)

### 6-Month Review (if Active/Light support)

**Formal Review Meeting (2h):**
- Where are KPIs vs targets? (6-month data)
- Has adoption sustained? (or declining?)
- Are champions still engaged?
- Is there capability to sustain without support?

**Outcomes:**
- Transition to zero support (if sustainable)
- Continue ongoing support (if value clear)
- Escalate (if slipping)

### Annual Check-In (12 months post)

**Email or Exploratory Call:**
- "How has the transformation held up?"
- "Any new challenges we can help with?"
- "Ready for the next chapter?"
- Opportunity for refresh or new initiatives
