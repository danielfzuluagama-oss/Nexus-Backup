## 2. Definición y Alcance

El Bootcamp BC-05 EMPRESAS capacita equipos en crear apps internas y validar nuevas ideas rápidamente usando no-code, reduciendo dependency en engineers y acelerando innovation cycles. Metodología 10/20/70: 10% MetodologIA, 20% CPO/innovation lead cliente, 70% facilitador no-code architect.

**Alcance específico:**
- Audit de high-impact applications que pueden ser no-code
- Design + prototyping: From concept to MVP en 1-2 semanas
- Database + integrations: Internal data sources, third-party APIs
- Deployment: Internal or customer-facing apps, scalability planning
- Innovation playbook: How to rapidly validate ideas post-bootcamp
- Metrics + dashboards: Usage, adoption, business impact measurement

1. **Speed multiplied:** 3-5 apps en 3 weeks vs. 3-6 meses traditional

3. **Validation engine:** Rapidly test 10+ ideas/quarter, fail fast, learn faster
4. **Team empowerment:** PM + business can build (less bottleneck en engineers)

**Ventaja vs. alternatives:**

**Infraestructura:**
- Plataforma: Zoom (sesiones) + Slack (daily, integration practice)
- No-code tools: Bubble, FlutterFlow, Airtable (client data sandbox)

**Pre-bootcamp:**

- App audit + prioritization (12h): Map problems, select 5 candidates, business logic
- Strategic workshop (6h): Innovation velocity goals, success metrics, sustainability

**Durante bootcamp:**
- 3 sesiones semanales (4-5h): Teórico (15%) + building (85%)
- Sesiones 1:1 (5 x 1h): Architecture reviews, design decisions, troubleshooting
- Tareas asincrónicas: Live building, testing, documentation, user feedback loops

**Post-bootcamp:**
- Sustainment workshop: 1 sesión (how team maintains apps)
- Rapid prototyping coaching: Monthly (guiding next 5 ideas)
- Support SLA: <24h critical, <1 week enhancements

**Recursos MetodologIA:**
- 1 no-code architect (80% dedicación)
- 1 innovation strategist (50% dedicación)
- 1 PM (100% dedicación)
