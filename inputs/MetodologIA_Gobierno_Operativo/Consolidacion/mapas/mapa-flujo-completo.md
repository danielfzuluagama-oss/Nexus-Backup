# MAPA DE FLUJO COMPLETO - Journey End-to-End

**Versión:** 1.0
**Generado:** 2026-03-24
**Propósito:** Visualización completa del ecosistema comercial con interconexiones
**Audience:** Líderes Comerciales, Analistas de Procesos, Especialistas de Operaciones
**Alcance:** 3 vertientes (B2B, B2C, GTM) + Intersecciones

---

## SÍNTESIS EJECUTIVA

Este documento mapea el flujo completo de cada vertiente comercial y muestra dónde se intersectan para generar oportunidades de cross-selling y crecimiento. Visualiza:

1. **B2B Flujo:** Señal inicial → QBR & Renewal
2. **B2C Flujo:** Evento de vida → Loop de referrals
3. **GTM Flujo:** Evaluación de aliado → Co-venta y co-marketing
4. **Intersecciones:** Dónde los flujos se cruzan generando valor exponencial

### Detalle Fase por Fase

#### FASE 1: SCOUTING (Prospecting & Account Planning)

```
[SEÑAL INICIAL]
    ↓
    ├─ Cuenta inbound (demo request, referral)
    ├─ Prospecting activo (cold email, LinkedIn, evento)
    ├─ Alerta de trigger (funding, hiring, cambio C-suite)
    └─ Referral de aliado GTM
         ↓
[RITUAL 1: Prospecting]
    • Identificar señal
    • Validar fuente
    • Registrar en CRM
    ↓
[RITUAL 2: Account Planning]
    • Mapear org chart
    • Identificar stakeholders
    • Definir buyer journey
    ↓
[RITUAL 3: Research Deep Dive]
    • Financials (presupuesto indicativo)
    • Tech stack actual
    • Competidores / proveedores
    ↓
[RITUAL 4: Cold Outreach]
    • Email personalizado (3 temas)
    • LinkedIn connection + message
    • Phone call (después de 3-5 dias)
    ↓
[RITUAL 5: First Meeting Booked]
    • 30 min descubierta inicial
    • Objetivo: Calendario discovery
    ↓
(GATE G1: ICP Qualification)
    ├─ SI (Score > 70)
    │  ↓
    │ [PASA A DISCOVERY]
    │
    ├─ NEUTRAL (Score 50-70)
    │  ↓
    │ [ENRICH: Get more data]

    │
    └─ NO (Score < 50)
       ↓

[RITUAL 12: Executive Alignment]

    ↓

[RITUAL 14: Market Ready QA]

    ├─ SI (COO aprueba margen)

    │ [PASA A GATE G4]

```
[PROPUESTA ENVIADA: Customer reviewing]

    • Quick wins planning
    • Onboarding timeline agreed
    ↓
(GATE G5: Deal Health Check)
    ├─ SI (Healthy: Sponsor engaged, timeline clear)
    │  ↓

    ├─ AMBAR (At risk: Sponsor disengaged OR timeline slipping)

    │ [INTENSIFY: Weekly check-ins]

    └─ ROJO (Dead: Sponsor has left OR budget denied)

```

**Métricas de Cierre:**

#### FASE 5: RETENCIÓN (Activation & QBR Cycle)

```
[CONTRATO FIRMADO: Activación iniciada]
    ↓
[RITUAL 18: Activation & Quick Win]
    • Setup rápido (72 horas primer ambiente)
    • First quick win (pequeña victoria que demuestre valor)
    • Entrenamiento de power users
    • Baseline metrics establecida
    ↓
(GATE G6: Activation Gate)
    ├─ SI (Quick win achieved, users trained)
    │  ↓
    │ [PASA A CUSTOMER SUCCESS MODE]
    │
    └─ NO (Bloqueado o delay)
       ↓
     [ESCALADA: Engineering + CSM]
     [RESCUE: Intensive support]

[CS MODE: Customer Success takes over]
    ↓
[RITUAL 19: Health Scoring (Monthly)]
    • Usage metrics (login frequency, feature adoption)
    • Business metrics (ROI tracking vs. baseline)
    • Sentiment (NPS, support tickets, escalations)
    • Scoring: Green (healthy) / Amber (at risk) / Red (zombie)
    ↓
(GATE G7: Health Score Review)
    ├─ SI (Green: Using product, seeing ROI)
    │  ↓
    │ [EXPANSION OPPORTUNITY]
    │ [Upsell or cross-sell planning]
    │ [Schedule QBR in 90 days]
    │
    ├─ AMBAR (At risk: Low usage OR missing ROI)
    │  ↓
    │ [RESCUE PROTOCOL]
    │ [AE + CS joint call]
    │ [New quick wins or change champion]
    │
    └─ ROJO (Zombie: No usage, no ROI contact)
       ↓
     [ALERT: May churn]
     [Intensive engagement or winback]

[QBR: Quarterly Business Review (every 90 days)]
    ↓
[RITUAL 20: QBR Meeting]
    • Business review (progress vs. goals)
    • New use cases discovered (expansion)
    • Roadmap alignment
    • Next quarter planning
    ↓
(GATE G8: Renewal Readiness)
    ├─ SI (QBR positive, customer happy)
    │  ↓
    │ [RENEWAL + EXPAND]
    │ [Schedule renewal 90 days before end]
    │ [Plan upsell for next fiscal year]
    │
    └─ NO (QBR negative, ROI not met)
       ↓
     [REMEDIATION: 30-day intensive support]
     [RESTART quick wins]
     [NEXT QBR: Decision point for renewal]
```

**Métricas de Retención:**
- % customers Green health score
- NPS B2B target: >50 (promoters)
- Expansion rate (% customers upsold): %
- Churn rate: % (target: <5%)
- Customer lifetime value (CLV): $

#### FASE 2: CALIFICACIÓN EMOCIONAL (Lead Scoring & Emotional Mapping)

```
[LEAD CONTACTED: Initial engagement]
    ↓
[RITUAL 2: Emotional Anchor Mapping]
    • Escuchar: ¿Qué emoción genera el evento?
    • Diagnóstico: ¿Miedo? ¿Excitación? ¿Estrés?
    • Anchor point: ¿Cuál es el deseo/problema principal?
    • Envisionamiento: ¿Cómo sería el estado ideal?
    ↓
[RITUAL 3: Lead Scoring + Friction Audit]
    • Scoring: Life event relevancia (40%) + Engagement (30%) + Fricción identificada (30%)
    • Fórmula: Score = (Event*0.4) + (Engagement*0.3) + (100-Friction*0.3)
    • Threshold: >60 = Qualified | 40-60 = Nurture | <40 = Monitor
    • Fricción: ¿Qué obstáculos percibe?
    ↓
(GATE G2: Lead Score Qualification)
    ├─ SI (Score > 60, emotional anchor mapped)
    │  ↓
    │ [PASA A NURTURE SECUENCIA]
    │
    ├─ NEUTRAL (Score 40-60)
    │  ↓
    │ [NURTURE: Email sequence genérica]
    │ [RETRY in 30 days]
    │
    └─ NO (Score < 40, weak signal)
       ↓
     [MONITOR: Tag for future re-engagement]
     [RETRY in 90 days if new signal]
```

**Métricas de Calificación:**
- Conversion life event → qualified lead: %
- Promedio lead score de qualified leads: (target: >65)
- Emotinal anchors mapped per cohort: % leads

```

    ↓
[RITUAL 6: Engagement Monitoring]

    └─ NO (Low engagement after 30 days)
       ↓

     [RETRY: New hook email]
       ↓
     After retry:
         └─ SI → [PASA A CIERRE]
         └─ NO → [NURTURE LONG-TERM: Monthly touchpoint]
                 [RETRY in 60 days with new angle]
```

**Métricas de Nurture:**
- Engagement rate por secuencia: % (target: >30%)
- Duración nurture → aha moment: días (target: 21-35)
- Aha moment detection accuracy: % predictions converted

```
[PARTNER PROSPECT IDENTIFICADO]

    ↓

    └─ NO (No pipeline or SLA conflicts)
