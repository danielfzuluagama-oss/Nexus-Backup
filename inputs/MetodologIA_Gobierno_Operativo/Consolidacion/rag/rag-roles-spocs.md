## SECCIÓN 1: ROLES DEL PROCESO COMERCIAL

### ROLES B2B - CLIENTE

Estos roles existen en la organización del cliente.

#### Economic Buyer (EB)
- **Definición:** Stakeholder con autoridad de presupuesto y responsabilidad financiera
- **Ubicación Típica:** CFO, VP Finance, Director Finance, VP Operations
- **Criterios de Identificación:**
  - Autoridad de firmar cheques sobre $100K+
  - Responsable de ROI del proyecto
  - Tiene P&L accountability
  - Participa en finanzas importantes
- **Interés:** ROI, TCO, payback period, financial risk
- **Engagement:**
  - Conversaciones en nivel de negocio
  - Business case reviews
  - ROI modeling
  - Contract approval
- **Señales de Interés:**
  - Pregunta sobre pricing
  - Participa en financial review
  - Aprueba budget allocation
- **Riesgo:** Si no está alineado, deal puede bloquearse
- **Estrategia de Venta:**
  - Lead with value/ROI
  - Bring financial comparison to alternatives
  - Relate to his KPIs (margin, efficiency, cost reduction)
  - Prepare executive brief
  - Be ready for budget cycle timing
- **Fase Crítica:** Discovery, Structuring, Signature
- **Métrica de Éxito:** EB confirms ROI >150%, presupuesto aprobado

#### Technical Steward (TS)
- **Definición:** Stakeholder técnico responsable de evaluación y implementación
- **Ubicación Típica:** CTO, VP Engineering, VP IT, Chief Architect
- **Criterios de Identificación:**
  - Responsable de tecnología stacky evaluación
  - Controla infrastructure decisions
  - Participa en arquitectura
  - Influencia en selection de vendors
- **Interés:** Technical fit, integration, scalability, security, maintainability
- **Engagement:**
  - Technical deep dives
  - Architecture discussions
  - Security reviews
  - Implementation planning
  - POC management
- **Señales de Interés:**
  - Solicita arquitectura detallada
  - Organiza technical proof of concept
  - Revisa security documentation
  - Participa en technical demos
- **Riesgo:** Si rechaza técnicamente, deal bloquea
- **Estrategia de Venta:**
  - Proveer arquitectura detallada
  - Demostrar escalabilidad
  - Abordar security/compliance
  - Facilitar POC/testing
  - Responder a preguntas técnicas profundas
- **Fase Crítica:** Discovery, Structuring, Implementation
- **Métrica de Éxito:** TS aprueba arquitectura, valida integration approach

#### User Champion (UC)
- **Definición:** Usuario final que aboga internamente por solución
- **Ubicación Típica:** Gerentes de departamento, power users, project owners
- **Criterios de Identificación:**
  - Usa solución día a día
  - Entiende pain point que resolvemos
  - Tiene influencia en departamento
  - Early advocate/promoter
- **Interés:** Usability, features para pain point, training, adoption
- **Engagement:**
  - Use case discovery
  - Feature requirements gathering
  - Training y enablement
  - Change management
  - Adoption facilitation
- **Señales de Interés:**
  - Participa entusiastamente en demostraciones
  - Identifica casos de uso específicos
  - Comparte con colegas
  - Lidera testeo
- **Riesgo:** Si no hay champion, adopción débil
- **Estrategia de Venta:**
  - Identificar early
  - Involucrar en demos
  - Hacer champion happy (mejor UX, features)
  - Involucrar en implementación
  - Usar como referencia interna
- **Fase Crítica:** Discovery, Implementation, Success
- **Métrica de Éxito:** UC activamente promoviendo, driving adoption

#### Sponsor
- **Definición:** Ejecutivo de alto nivel que patrocina iniciativa
- **Ubicación Típica:** CEO, CDOO, VP ejecutivo, board member
- **Criterios de Identificación:**
  - Reportan directamente a CEO
  - Control de presupuesto estratégico
  - Responsable de strategic initiative
  - Tiene air cover ejecutivo
- **Interés:** Alineación estratégica, impacto de negocio, timeline
- **Engagement:**
  - Executive briefing
  - Strategic alignment discussion
  - Resource commitment
  - Escalation support
  - Executive steering committee
- **Señales de Interés:**
  - Participa en initial meeting
  - Asigna recursos
  - Facilita remoción de blockers
  - Participa en steering
- **Riesgo:** Sin sponsor, iniciativa puede ser deprioritized
- **Estrategia de Venta:**
  - Connect a strategic goals
  - Get introduction
  - Prepare brief executiva
  - Demostrar impacto estratégico
  - Facilitar regular communication
- **Fase Crítica:** Discovery, Signature, Implementation
- **Métrica de Éxito:** Sponsor activamente supporting, removing blockers

### ROLES B2B - VENDOR (NUESTRO LADO)

#### Sales Development Representative (SDR)
- **Definición:** Especialista en prospecting y qualification
- **Reporting:** VP Sales Development o VP Sales
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Cold outreach (email, calls, LinkedIn)
  - Initial qualification (ICP fit)
  - Lead scoring
  - Appointment setting
  - Initial discovery of needs
  - Lead quality management
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Calls/dials per day
  - Email sequences per week
  - Response rate target
  - Meetings booked target
  - SQL conversion rate (e.g., 5-10%)
  - Qualification accuracy
- **KPIs:**
  - Activity (dials, emails, touches)
  - Response rate
  - Meetings set
  - SQL conversion rate
  - Deal progression from their meetings
  - Lead quality metric
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Initial qualification, handoff to AE
- **Success Factor:** High activity, good qualification, smooth handoff
- **Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, email sequences, CRM, phone system
- **Reportes a:** Manager semanal, forecast biweekly
- **Ciclo Diario:** Morning standup, prospecting, follow-ups, reporting
- **Capacitación:** Selling methodology, product knowledge, objection handling, qualification framework

#### Account Executive (AE)
- **Definición:** Vendedor responsable de ciclo de venta completo
- **Reporting:** Sales Manager o VP Sales
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - End-to-end deal management
  - Prospect qualification (BANT+)
  - Discovery conversations
  - Solution design (con presales)
  - Proposal management
  - Negotiation
  - Relationship management
  - Forecast accuracy
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Annual quota (typically $500K-$5M+ ARR)
  - Quarterly incremental
  - Win rate target
  - Average deal size target
  - Sales cycle target
  - Forecast accuracy
- **KPIs:**
  - Quota attainment %
  - Win rate
  - Average contract value (ACV)
  - Sales cycle (days to close)
  - Pipeline coverage (3x quota)
  - Forecast accuracy
  - Customer satisfaction (NPS, CSM feedback)
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Ownership end-to-end hasta signature
- **Success Factor:** Consultative selling, relationship building, forecast accuracy, deal progression
- **Tools:** CRM, email, phone, sales enablement platform
- **Reportes a:** Weekly 1-on-1 con manager, weekly forecast
- **Ciclo Semanal:** Account planning, discovery calls, proposal reviews, negotiation
- **Capacitación:** Selling methodology, product deep knowledge, negotiation skills, customer insights

#### Sales Manager
- **Definición:** Manager de equipo de AEs
- **Reporting:** VP Sales o SVP Sales
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Manage 6-8 AEs typically
  - Forecast accuracy
  - Quota attainment
  - Coaching and development
  - Hiring and retention
  - Pipeline management
  - Deal review and escalation
  - Customer relationships (strategic)
  - Sales operations coordination
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Team quota (aggregate)
  - Team win rate
  - Team forecast accuracy
  - Retention targets
- **KPIs:**
  - Team quota attainment
  - Team win rate
  - Team average cycle time
  - Team forecast accuracy
  - Team member retention
  - Team member ramp time
  - New hire productivity
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Escalation, coaching, major deals ($1M+)
- **Success Factor:** Team leadership, forecast discipline, deal guidance, talent development
- **Reportes a:** Weekly team forecast, monthly business review, coaching activities
- **Ciclo Semanal:** Team meetings, 1-on-1s, deal reviews, forecast calls
- **Capacitación:** Leadership, coaching, territory planning, forecasting

#### Sales Engineer (SE)
- **Definición:** Técnico que apoya sales con arquitectura y validación
- **Reporting:** VP Sales Engineering o VP Solutions
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Technical discovery
  - Solution design/architecture
  - Product demonstrations
  - POC management
  - RFP/RFI technical responses
  - Technology deep dives
  - Objection handling (technical)
  - Implementation planning
  - Customer success support (pre-sales)
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Deals supported
  - POC success rate
  - Deal velocity (speed through sales cycle)
  - POC closure rate
  - RFP win rate
  - Proposal accuracy
- **KPIs:**
  - POC-to-deal conversion rate
  - Deal velocity impact
  - Customer technical satisfaction
  - Proposal quality (first-pass acceptance)
  - Time spent per deal stage
  - Technical risk mitigation
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Technical solution, validation, RFP response
- **Success Factor:** Technical credibility, solution design clarity, customer education
- **Tools:** Product knowledge, architecture tools, demo environment, lab resources
- **Reportes a:** Weekly deal reviews, monthly technical metrics
- **Ciclo:** Technical sessions, demos, POCs, implementation planning
- **Capacitación:** Product architecture, technical certifications, customer success patterns

#### Customer Success Manager (CSM)
- **Definición:** Responsable de adopción, valor y retención post-firma
- **Reporting:** VP Customer Success o VP Operations
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Onboarding management
  - Implementation coordination
  - Training y enablement
  - Value realization
  - Health monitoring
  - QBR facilitation
  - Expansion opportunity identification
  - Renewal management
  - Issue escalation
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Retention rate by customer segment
  - Net revenue retention
  - Expansion pipeline
  - Customer satisfaction (NPS)
  - Health score targets
  - Adoption metrics
- **KPIs:**
  - Customer retention rate
  - Net revenue retention
  - Customer health scores
  - NPS scores
  - Expansion revenue
  - Upsell closure rate
  - Time-to-value
  - Adoption rate
  - Customer satisfaction
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Post-signature onboarding, ongoing health, renewal
- **Success Factor:** Proactive engagement, value articulation, relationship building
- **Tools:** CSM platform, health score dashboard, CRM
- **Reportes a:** Monthly account reviews, QBR feedback
- **Ciclo:** Onboarding, regular check-ins, QBRs, expansion planning
- **Capacitación:** Product knowledge, customer success methodology, business acumen

#### Presales/Solution Consultant
- **Definición:** Consultor que ayuda definir solución
- **Reporting:** VP Sales Engineering o VP Presales
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Requirements gathering
  - Solution architecture
  - Gap analysis
  - Business case development
  - Proposal development
  - Technical specifications
  - Implementation approach definition
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Deals supported
  - Proposal quality
  - Business case accuracy
  - Solution design acceptance
- **KPIs:**
  - Proposal-to-deal conversion rate
  - Business case accuracy vs actual
  - Solution design acceptance rate
  - Implementation smooth transition
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Solution design, business case, specifications
- **Success Factor:** Consultative approach, business acumen, technical credibility
- **Ciclo:** Discovery support, design phases, proposal development
- **Capacitación:** Business case modeling, solution architecture, industry knowledge

#### Account Manager (Mid-market/Enterprise)
- **Definición:** Expandir y retener cuentas estratégicas
- **Reporting:** VP Account Management o VP Sales
- **Responsabilidades (similares a CSM pero con enfoque expansión):**
  - Account planning
  - Expansion opportunity identification
  - Executive relationship building
  - QBR facilitation
  - Upsell/cross-sell
  - Renewal negotiations
  - Vertical expansion
  - Customer strategic alignment
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Expansion revenue target
  - Renewal rate
  - Net revenue retention
  - Upsell closure rate
  - Account growth
- **KPIs:**
  - Expansion revenue
  - Net revenue retention
  - Upsell closure rate
  - Customer satisfaction
  - Account growth trajectory
  - Retention rate
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Expansion, renewal, account health
- **Success Factor:** Strategic thinking, relationship building, value discovery
- **Ciclo:** Account planning, QBRs, opportunity identification, expansion deals
- **Diferencia vs CSM:** AM más enfocado en expansión/revenue; CSM en adopción/success

#### Deal Manager
- **Definición:** Especialista en deal structure, negotiation, compliance
- **Reporting:** VP Sales o VP Deal Management
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Deal structure consulting
  - Financial terms review
  - Contract negotiation support
  - Legal coordination
  - Compliance checks
  - Discount justification
  - Special terms approval
  - Post-signature fulfilment coordination
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Deal speed-to-close
  - Deal profitability
  - Contract compliance
  - Negotiation efficiency
- **KPIs:**
  - Average days to signature from proposal
  - Profitability by deal
  - Contract terms adherence
  - Discount utilization %
  - Customer satisfaction with terms
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Structure, negotiation, compliance
- **Success Factor:** Deal structure creativity, negotiation skill, financial acumen
- **Ciclo:** Post-proposal, negotiation phase, contract finalization
- **Interacción:** Con Legal, Finance, Customer, Sales

#### VP Sales
- **Definición:** Chief sales officer/leader de organización de ventas
- **Reporting:** Chief Revenue Officer o CEO
- **Responsabilidades:**
  - Sales strategy and planning
  - Quota setting
  - Territory management
  - Hiring and talent management
  - Sales culture
  - Forecast accuracy
  - Revenue targets
  - Large deal oversight
  - Customer relationships (C-level)
  - Sales operations coordination
  - Sales enablement oversight
- **Objetivos/Quotas:**
  - Annual revenue target
  - Team quota attainment
  - Team win rate
  - Forecast accuracy
  - Retention targets
  - New customer acquisition
- **KPIs:**
  - Total revenue
  - Quota attainment %
  - Win rate
  - Average deal size
  - Sales cycle
  - Forecast accuracy
  - Team retention
  - New hire productivity
  - Pipe generation
- **Responsabilidad por Deal:** Large deals ($1M+), major accounts, escalation
- **Success Factor:** Strategic vision, leadership, sales discipline, customer focus
- **Reportes a:** Monthly with CFO/CRO, quarterly with Board
- **Interacción:** CEO, Finance, Customer Success, Product, Marketing
