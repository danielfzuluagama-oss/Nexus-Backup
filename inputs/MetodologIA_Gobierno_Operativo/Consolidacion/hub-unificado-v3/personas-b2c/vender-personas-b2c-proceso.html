1. PROPÓSITO DEL PROCESO ▼

Convertir profesionales individuales en alumnos pagantes mediante la orquestación de 5 etapas interdependientes que detectan ciclos de vida, mapean drivers emocionales, entregan valor inmediato, nutren relaciones y activan loops de referencia viral. El proceso es emoción-céntrico (no feature-céntrico): vendemos aspiración, identidad y transformación.

LÍMITES 2. SCOPE Y LÍMITES ▼

Alcance

Profesionales independientes, empleados en transición, emprendedores early-stage (edad 28-55)
Plataformas: LinkedIn, email, Instagram, TikTok (según cohort)
Value prop: upskilling, repositioning, identity amplification
Budget típico: $1K-$10K anuales
Sales cycle: 30-90 días

Límites Explícitos

NO incluye prospectos B2B (empresas ≥50 empleados) — ruta Enterprise
NO incluye menores de edad o jubilados (fuera del rango etario clave)
NO incluye ventas inbound puras sin "life event" detectable
NO incluye remarketing post-rechazo explícito (>2 "no gracias" consecutivos)
NO incluye países sin poder adquisitivo (LATAM, MENA, South Asia clasificados por TCM)
3. FLUJO MACRO DEL PROCESO ▼

Etapa 1: SCOUTING - Detectar Life Events

Etapa 3: STRUCTURING - Oferta Valor Inmediato

SUPUESTOS 5. SUPUESTOS EXPLÍCITOS DEL PROCESO ▼
1Los cambios de vida generan urgencia emocional: Un life event (despido, promoción, burnout) crea ventana de 14-30 días de máxima apertura.
2La venta B2C es venta de aspiración, no features: Prospecto compra versión futura de sí mismo (identidad, status, red).
3El ancla emocional es predecible: 80% de prospectos = 3-4 categorías (fear of irrelevance, impostor syndrome, status anxiety, network isolation).
4El micro-offer crea habituación: Micro-offer (free workshop, 30-min consult) = 60%+ conversión a programa en 30 días. Sin micro-offer = 15%.
5LinkedIn es principal señal de life events: 75% eventos documentables públicamente. Email + phone = confirmación, no descubrimiento.
6El referral loop es el diferenciador: Con referral loops activos = CAC cae 40-60% en cohorts maduros.
7La fricción mata conversiones B2C: Signup 4+ campos = 40% abandonment. 1-click = 80%+ completion.
8Voice calibration > contenido: Slack-tone engagement = 3-5x mejor que academicese.

EDGE CASES 7. CASOS BORDE (EDGE CASES) ▼

Caso 1: Prospecto con Emotional Anchor muy profundo

Trigger: Severe burnout, mental health red flags.

Acción: Pause nurture. Success Manager support. Free intro workshop. 2+ conversaciones antes de structuring.

Caso 2: Multi-touch non-responder

Trigger: 3+ emails + 2+ LinkedIn, 14+ days, zero engagement.

Acción: Move to hibernation. Reactive trigger on status change. Revisit 6-month mark.

Caso 3: Highly motivated, zero budget

Trigger: Strong anchor, high engagement, "no money now".

Acción: Payment plan (3 cuotas) O affiliate referral O waitlist con early-bird.

Caso 4: Free consultation seeker

Trigger: 3+ free calls, heavy content engagement, no micro-offer acceptance.

Acción: Post-2nd call: "$299 VIP bundle". Si rechaza → nurture pool, no mas free calls.

Caso 5: Employer benefit access

Trigger: Already has free access via employer.

Acción: Premium upgrade ($1.5K) O corporate account manager route.

Caso 6: Influencer, no payment history

Trigger: 10K+ followers, pide "partnership".

Acción: Si NPS ≥9 → affiliate deal (15-20%). Si <9 → micro-payment ($199).

Caso 7: Prospect regresa post-rechazo

Trigger: 6+ months post-"no", re-engagement.

Acción: "Something shifted?" New case study + free 15-min call. Reset frequency.

DISEÑO 8. DECISIONES DE DISEÑO CLAVE ▼

Decisión 1: Life Events + LinkedIn Signals

Rationale: Life event + warm = 35-40% open rates. LinkedIn signals = 60% relevance.

Implication: Sales = pull (detect + react), not push.

Decisión 2: Mandatory 1:1 Discovery

Rationale: Emotional anchor mapping needs bidirectional conversation. 20-30 min call = 70%+ qualified.

Implication: SDRs book calls; closers clarify. No bypass.

Decisión 3: Micro-offer within 24h

Rationale: Momentum critical. 24h delay = 40% drop-off.

Implication: Pre-template offers; Calendly mandatory.

Decisión 4: Conversational voice

Rationale: B2C tired of corporate speak. Slack-tone = 3-5x engagement.

Implication: Sales copy audit quarterly.

Decisión 5: 1-click enrollment

Rationale: B2C deal-sensitive. Friction = abandonment.

Implication: CRM post-payment; forms deferred to onboarding.

ANTI-PATTERNS 9. ANTI-PATTERNS (QUÉ NO HACER) ▼
1Anti-pattern: Prospecting sin Life Event signal
Why fails: 97% irrelevance
Alternate: Only within 14 days of LinkedIn signal
2Anti-pattern: Discovery call con pitch (instead of mapping)
Why fails: Felt like sales call
Alternate: 80% listening, 20% validation
3Anti-pattern: Micro-offer genérico
Why fails: 10% vs 60% conversion
Alternate: Template + 2-3 custom hooks per anchor
4Anti-pattern: Nurturing = email dumps
Why fails: >5% unsubscribe
Alternate: 3 emails/2 weeks, conversational
5Anti-pattern: Closing door post-"no"
Why fails: "no now" ≠ "no ever"
Alternate: Nurture pool; re-activate on trigger
6Anti-pattern: Success = completion % only
Why fails: Completes pero NPS 3/10 = negative referrals
Alternate: Completion ≥70% + NPS ≥8 + referral activation
FALLBACKS 10. FALLBACKS OPERATIVOS ▼

Fallback 1: Discovery call no-show

Plan A: Auto-reminder + reinvite (4h post)

Plan B (24h): "3 time slots next week — what works?"

Plan C (48h): Async: "Video intro + Q&A form"

Plan D (72h+): Move to nurture; no re-scheduling after 2 no-shows

Recovery: 30-40% saved

Fallback 2: Micro-offer no-show

Plan A (24h): "Tech issue? Re-recording available."

Plan B (48h): Async recorded content + Slack group

Plan C (5d): 1:1 office hours (5-min slot)

Plan D (7d+): Nurture pool; offer expired

Recovery: 25-35% convert

Fallback 3: Email list stale (>5% bounce)

Plan A: Re-verification ("Still here?" single email)

Plan B: Fresh LinkedIn data; update emails

Plan C: Segment by engagement; pause 90+ days dormant

Plan D: Survey subset; iterate copy

Recovery: 10-15% rehabilitation

Fallback 4: Nurturing conversion flat (<8%)

Plan A: A/B test subject lines (3 variants)

Plan B: Trial cadence (1x/week vs 3x/2weeks)

Plan C: Segment by life event; 3 micro-offer variants

Plan D: Add video preview

Recovery: 40-60% improvement

Fallback 5: Post-enrollment churn

Plan A: Onboarding NPS <5? Success Manager 48h

Plan B: Missing 2 sessions? SMS reminder

Plan C: Low Week 1? Cohort buddy or small-group calls

Plan D: If churn confirmed, next cohort + 20% discount

Recovery: 25-35% save rate
