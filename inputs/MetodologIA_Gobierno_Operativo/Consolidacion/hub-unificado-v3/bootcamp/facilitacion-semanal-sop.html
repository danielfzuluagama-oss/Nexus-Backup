Identificación ▼
Código SOP: SOP-FS-BC
Proceso Padre: Entregar Bootcamp (Ver: entregar-bootcamp-proceso.md)
Fase: Facilitación Semanal (T-0 a T+15, 3 semanas × 5 días/semana)
Responsable Principal: Facilitador Principal (teaching, timing, energy, culture)
Responsable Secundario: Facilitador Asistente (squad mentoring, tech support, data capture)
Stakeholders: Tech Ops (infrastructure), Ops (attendance), Participantes
Input desde: SOP-OC-BC (cohort ready, squads formed)
Output hacia: SOP-GRAD-BC (Graduación & Alumni)
Gate: Weekly milestone delivery (Friday showcases, min 80% on-track)
Objetivo Binario ▼

Ejecutar 3 semanas intensivas manteniendo:

SUPUESTOS Supuestos & Límites ▼

Supuestos:

1Facilitador Lead está presente 100% (no delegable a asistente)
2Squads formadas pre-bootcamp (ver SOP-OC-BC)
3Curriculum finalized + lesson plans ready
4Tech platform (Slack, Notion, Zoom) stable + monitored
5Participants committed 40-50h/week (no part-time feasible)

Límites (No cubierto):

Personal crises (family, health) - handled by Facilitador as pastoral care, not curriculum
Paid implementation support post-bootcamp
Career coaching (separate alumni product)
Estructura Semanal Repetible ▼

Cada semana sigue el mismo patrón (Monday → Friday):

MONDAY: Kick-off + Context
└─ Facilitador presenta learning objectives + project milestone
└─ Duración: 90 min

TUESDAY-THURSDAY: Skill-building Sessions + Squad Work
└─ 60 min skill-building session (todos)
└─ 90 min squad work time (unsupervised)
└─ Asistente disponible office hours
└─ Duración: 150 min total

FRIDAY: Showcase + Retro
└─ Squads presentan progreso (5 min each)
└─ Cohort retrospective (20 min)
└─ Duración: 60 min
Daily Facilitation Routines ▼

MONDAY: Kick-off Session (90 min)

Facilitador Role: Solo (Principal)

Objetivo: Inspire for the week, clarify learning objectives, set project milestone expectations

Agenda:

TimeActivityNotes
0-5 minOpeningEnthusiasm, thank you for showing up
5-15 minRecapWhat did we learn last week? Where are we?
15-30 minContextWhy does this week's topic matter? Stories.
30-60 minLearning objectivesHere's what you'll be able to do by Friday
60-80 minProject milestoneThis is what you'll build/deliver by Friday
80-90 minSquad breakoutSprint planning (5 min per squad, Asistente facil)

Key Talking Points:

"This week builds on last week"
"By Friday, each squad will have [specific artifact]"
"You have the skills; now apply them"

Energy: High, motivational, specific

TUESDAY-THURSDAY: Skill-Building Session (60 min)

Facilitador Role: Teaching + live demo + Q&A

Agenda:

TimeActivityFacilitator RoleParticipant Role
0-3 minHookTell a story or ask questionEngage
3-20 minTeachExplain concept + show examplesTake notes, listen
20-45 minDemo + PracticeLive demo, then guide hands-onBuild alongside facilitador
45-55 minApply"How would you use this in your project?"Think, discuss in squads
55-60 minAssignAsync work for squad timeNote next steps

Facilitation Principles:

Live, not polished: Build in real-time, make mistakes (it's OK)
Question-driven: Ask questions more than making statements
Narrative: Connect to participant context (their projects)
Interactive: Poll, vote, hands-up often

Post-session:

Upload recording to Notion (within 2 hours)
Post async learning materials to Slack (resources, templates, FAQs)

TUESDAY-THURSDAY: Async Squad Work (90 min)

Facilitador Role: Circulation, mentoring, monitoring

Pasos:

1Squad work starts immediately after session:
Squads break into separate rooms (or Slack channels if remote)
Task: Apply today's learning to their project
Facilitador Lead + Asistente(s) circulate/drop into squad rooms
2Facilitador actions during squad work:
Listen (first 10 min): What are they discussing? Understand context
Ask probing questions: "What are you trying to solve?" "Why did you choose this approach?"
Don't answer directly: Guide them to their own answers
Identify struggles early: If stuck, nudge or pair with someone stronger
Celebrate progress: "That's smart thinking!"
3Asistente specific role:
Monitor engagement in Slack/chat
Respond to tech questions immediately
Document any struggles (for facilitador debrief)
Offer office hours: "Stuck? Drop into Zoom at 4pm for 1:1"
4Office hours (4-5pm, optional):
Facilitador Asistente available (15-30 min 1:1 sessions)
Help with blockers (tech issues, conceptual confusion, squad conflict)
Do NOT do work for them; coach them through

Output: Squad progresses on project, facilitador gathers data on who's struggling

FRIDAY: Showcase + Retrospective (60 min)

Facilitador Role: Emcee + coach feedback + culture builder

Agenda:

TimeActivityFacilitador RoleParticipant Role
0-5 minOpeningCelebrate the weekSettle, excitement
5-35 minSquad showcasesFacilitate 5 min per squad + Q&A (2 min)Present + listen to peers
35-50 minFeedbackModel good feedback (specific, actionable)Ask questions, give feedback
50-60 minRetroFacilitate discussionReflect + commit to improvements

Squad Showcase Format:

Pitch (2 min): What did you build? Why? How does it work?
Demo (2 min): Show it working
Q&A (1 min): Peers ask questions

Facilitador Feedback Model:

"I liked [specific thing]. Here's why..."
"What if you tried [idea]? How would that change [outcome]?"
"I'm curious about [design choice]. Walk me through your thinking"

Retrospective Prompts:

"What went well this week?"
"What was hard?"
"What will you do differently next week?"
"How's your squad dynamic?"

Closing (5 min):

"You shipped something real this week. That's incredible."
"Weekend optional deep-dive [if applicable]"
"See you Monday!"

Squad Management Protocols ▼

Squad Dynamics Monitoring

Daily signals (Facilitador watches Slack, breakout rooms):

Are all members speaking?
Is one person dominating?
Is there psychological safety (can people say "I'm lost")?

Weekly retro feedback:

Team satisfaction: "How's your squad feeling? 1-5"
Psychological safety: "Do you feel safe asking questions?"
Collaboration: "Is work fairly distributed?"

Conflict Resolution

Level 1 - Tension Emerges:

Facilitador notices in Slack or retro
Light comment to team: "I'm noticing some strong opinions; that's healthy. Let's make sure everyone feels heard"
Reframe as creative friction

Level 2 - Persistent Conflict:

Facilitador has 1:1 with each person (separate conversations):
"What's your perspective?"
"What do you need from the squad?"
"What's one thing you appreciate about [other person]?"
Facilitador synthesizes + facilitates team conversation

Level 3 - Breakdown:

Team conversation isn't working
Facilitador options:
Pair with mentor (external coach)
Rebalance squad (move one person to another squad, week 2 only)
Escalate to CEO Delivery (last resort)
Pace Management (Scope Adjustment) ▼

Common scenarios:

ScenarioSignalAction
Project too ambitiousMid-week: Squad realizes can't finishReduce scope (MVP only), defer stretch goals
Participants lost<50% understand conceptReteach (shorter, simpler), add 1:1 office hours
Squad stuck on tech>30 min on single tech issuePair with stronger person, or skip tech detail
Momentum lostAttendance drops, engagement lowLightbulb moment story, reset expectations

Principle: Protect learning + project quality. Scope is flexible.

Office Hours + 1:1 Support ▼

Schedule:

Tue-Thu: 4-5pm daily (30-min slots, first-come-first-served)
Participants book via Slack bot or shared calendar

Facilitador Asistente role:

Welcome person: "What's blocking you?"
Listen first: Don't jump to solving
Coach, don't solve: "What have you tried? What could you try?"
Document: Note in shared doc (for facilitador debrief)

Common office hours topics:

Tech issues (can't run script, API error)
Conceptual confusion (don't understand framework)
Squad conflict (need mediator)
Motivation check-in (tired, overwhelmed)

EDGE CASES Casos Borde (Facilitación Semanal) ▼

Caso 1: Participant formative assessment fail (<60% score) mid-week 2

Trigger: Quiz muestra que <50% saben concept

Decision: Reteach + boost:

Reschedule 1 skill session (compress otro)
Simplified explanation + concrete example
Office hours boost (1:1 with struggling folks)
Asistente makes extra content (video, cheatsheet)

Principle: Foundation must be solid before moving forward

Caso 2: Squad conflict tensión alta (not resolved by facilitador coaching)

Trigger: Squad miembro pide rebalance, o Slack muestra tension después de 1:1s

Decision: Actions:

Pair struggling person con mentor externo (si disponible)
Team reset conversation (Facilitador + squad, hour-long)
If still broken: Move person to another squad (week 2 only, can't move week 3)
Last resort: Escalate to CEO + coaching partner

Caso 3: Attendance drops drastically mid-week 2 (>2 people missing Mon/Fri)

Trigger: Múltiples personas no asistiendo sin aviso

Decision: Immediate:

Facilitador calls cada persona (root cause investigation)
Common answers: work crisis, motivation dip, personal
Targeted interventions:
Work crisis → negotiate flexibility (compress schedule, office hours extend)
Motivation dip → pair con mentor, highlight progress
Personal → offer pause + restart option

Escalate if multiple = cohort-wide issue

Caso 4: Tech failure mid-session (Zoom dies, live stream breaks)

Trigger: Platform outage durante skill session

Decision: Immediate fallback:

Pause 2 min, assess duration estimate
If <5 min: Wait + resume
If >5 min: Move to async (send recording later) + office hours for Q&A
Asistente troubleshoots backend

Reschedule if <50% of session lost

Caso 5: Capstone project scope explodes (squad realizes impossible week 2)

Trigger: "We'll never finish this in 1 week"

Decision: Early conversation (not week 3):

Facilitate scope reduction (MVP only)
Defer stretch goals to roadmap
Or pivot to demo (concept, no full product)
Reframe: "Learning > perfection. Ship what you can learn from."

Caso 6: Participant burnout signal (exhausted, disengaged week 2)

Trigger: Verbal cues ("I'm tired"), body language, or engagement drop

Decision: 1:1 conversation:

"I'm noticing fatigue. How are you actually?"
Validate struggle (bootcamp IS intense)
Options: lighter load for few days, or shift focus
Check if personal crisis (life support needed vs. bootcamp support)
ANTI-PATTERNS Anti-Patterns (QUÉ NO HACER) ▼
NO ignore assessment data (<70% pass) - reteach required
NO assume squad conflict resolves on own (facilitator must intervene)
NO abandon struggling participant (office hours + mentoring critical)
NO let scope creep (hold MVP line)
NO skip Friday retros (cohort health signal)
NO over-teach (leave 30% for squad independent work)
