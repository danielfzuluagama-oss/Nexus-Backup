2. Definición y Alcance ▼

El Bootcamp BC-05 EMPRESAS capacita equipos en crear apps internas y validar nuevas ideas rápidamente usando no-code, reduciendo dependency en engineers y acelerando innovation cycles. Metodología 10/20/70: 10% MetodologIA, 20% CPO/innovation lead cliente, 70% facilitador no-code architect.

Alcance específico:

Audit de high-impact applications que pueden ser no-code
Design + prototyping: From concept to MVP en 1-2 semanas
Database + integrations: Internal data sources, third-party APIs
Deployment: Internal or customer-facing apps, scalability planning
Innovation playbook: How to rapidly validate ideas post-bootcamp
Metrics + dashboards: Usage, adoption, business impact measurement
3. Audiencia del Segmento ▼

Perfil de empresa:

Sector: Tech, SaaS, Financial, Retail, Consultancy (knowledge-work heavy)
Tamaño: 100-500 personas
Contexto: Scaling innovation, internal tools backlog large, product teams stretched
Budget: COP 15-25M para innovation acceleration initiative

Stakeholders:

Sponsor: VP Product, CPO, VP Innovation, CIO
Facilitador interno: Product manager, innovation lead, architect
Participantes: 8-20 (product managers, business analysts, designers, some engineers)
Champion: 1-2 no-code builders para mantener ecosystem

Motivación:

Reduce time-to-product (weeks vs. months for ideas)
Free engineers (focus on core product, no distraction from internal tools)
Validate ideas faster (MVP in 2-3 weeks, measure before build)
Build internal tools para escalabilidad (CRM, reporting, automation)
Empower non-technical team members (democracy in building)
4. Propuesta de Valor Diferenciada ▼

Para la empresa:

1Speed multiplied: 3-5 apps en 3 weeks vs. 3-6 meses traditional
2Cost savings: 50-60% menos engineering hours para internal tools
3Validation engine: Rapidly test 10+ ideas/quarter, fail fast, learn faster
4Team empowerment: PM + business can build (less bottleneck en engineers)
5Innovation culture: "We can prototype anything" → competitive advantage

Ventaja vs. alternatives:

Mejor que hiring low-code vendors (COP 40-80M) → capabilidad interna
Mejor que consulting agencies → knowledge transfer, ownership
Mejor que tool-only (Zapier, Make) → proprietary apps, data control
5. Arquitectura Curricular (3 semanas) ▼

Semana 1: Assessment + No-code Fundamentals + Design Thinking

Lunes: Kickoff + audit de app candidates | Strategic alignment | Prioritization
Miércoles: Bubble + FlutterFlow fundamentals (visual-first approach) | First app: quick win
Viernes: Design thinking + rapid prototyping | Figma crash course | Mockup-to-code patterns

Semana 2: Advanced Features + Business Logic + Integrations

Lunes: Database design + data modeling (no SQL knowledge required)
Miércoles: Workflows + automations | API integrations | Payment processing
Viernes: Multi-app strategies | Enterprise features (permissions, audit logs, scaling)

Semana 3: Deployment + Validation + Innovation Playbook

Lunes: Deployment + infrastructure + monitoring
Miércoles: Metrics + analytics setup | MVP validation framework | Growth loops
Viernes: Demo ejecutivo + rapid innovation playbook | Roadmap (5 next ideas) + sustainable ecosystem
