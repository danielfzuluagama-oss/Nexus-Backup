Identificación ▼
Código SOP: SOP-FAC-WS
Proceso Padre: Entregar Workshop (Ver: entregar-workshop-proceso.md)
Fase: Facilitación (T-0: 3 horas exactas = 180 min)
Responsable Principal: Facilitador Principal (conducción + timing)
Responsable Secundario: Facilitador Asistente (documentación + soporte técnico)
Stakeholders: Coordinador Ops (logística en vivo), Participantes, Cliente (observador opcional)
Input desde: SOP-PREP-WS (deck, briefing, logistics), Rituals: Bienvenida, Co-creación, Commitment
Output hacia: SOP-CIERRE-WS (video, fotos, NPS, action cards)
Objetivo Binario ▼

Ejecutar 3 horas con éxito si:

Si alguno falla: documentar como "sesión de aprendizaje", no fracaso (data valuable para evolución)

SUPUESTOS Supuestos Explícitos ▼

Supuestos:

1Facilitador ha dormido ≥7h y comió (fatiga = baja facilitación)
2Asistente está presente y capacitado en captura de data (fotos, cards, engagement notes)
3Participantes tienen capacidad cognitiva suficiente para co-crear (no sesión de 8h previo agotador)
4Contexto cliente ha sido comunicado a facilitador (via briefing T-7)

Limitaciones (qué NO controlamos):

Motivación individual del participante (externo)
Disrupciones externas (llamada de jefe, emergencia)
Dinámicas de poder dentro de grupo (puede inhibir participación)
Pre-Session (T-60 min) ▼

Actividad 1: Arrival & Setup

Responsable: Facilitador + Ops Coordinator

Pasos:

1Arrive early (60 min antes de hora inicio):
Check venue setup (chairs, tables, AV, WiFi)
Test projector, sound system, microphone
Arrange seating (U-shape preferred for engagement)
Set up registration table if not done
2Tech test (15 min):
Load deck on laptop + projector
Test speaker audio (level, clarity)
Check recording (camera position, audio input)
Have backup: printed decks, handouts
3Material setup (10 min):
Lay out canvases on tables
Markers, sticky notes, pens ready
Name badges, nametags
Water, coffee, snacks visible
4Psyche prep (10 min):
Walk through room
Breathe deeply
Review briefing doc + key messages
Mentally rehearse opening (30 sec)

Actividad 2: Participant Reception (T-30 to T-0)

Pasos:

1Greet early arrivals at door:
Warm welcome, thank you for coming
Offer coffee, help with seating
Informal conversation (read energy of room)
25 min before start: Facilitator Asistente does roll call:
Check who is here vs. roster
Note absences (to share with Ops post)
Seat stragglers
3At T-0: Stand at front, make eye contact, smile
Ops stops accepting new entries
Recording starts
Session: Minute-by-Minute Framework ▼

BLOCK 1: BIENVENIDA & CONTEXTO (00:00 - 00:15) | 15 min

Facilitator Role: Solo (Principal)

Objective: Set tone, establish psychological safety, clarify objectives

Minute-by-Minute:

MinActivityFacilitator ActionParticipant Engagement
00:00-00:02WelcomeStand, big smile, greet by name if possibleSettle, attention
00:02-00:05IcebreakerShare 1 personal story (30 sec) + ask: "Who's here for first time?"Hands up; build intimacy
00:05-00:10ContextExplain workshop purpose, why today matters, what they'll leave withListen, nod

Key Lines:

"Today isn't a lecture; it's a journey we take together"
"There are no stupid questions—only insight waiting to happen"
"By the end of 3 hours, you'll have [X tool] + [Y framework] + [Z action plan"

BLOCK 2: MAPEAMIENTO DE REALIDAD ACTUAL (00:15 - 00:30) | 15 min

Facilitator Role: Dialogue (Q&A)

Objective: Surface where group is today; create baseline

Pasos:

1Polling (5 min): Ask 3-5 quick questions to understand current state:
"Who's used ChatGPT?" (hands)
"Who's used it for work?" (subset)
"What's one biggest challenge you're facing right now?" (popcorn responses, 1-2 min)
2Synthesis (5 min): Facilitator maps responses into themes:
"I'm hearing X, Y, Z as big themes"
"We're going to address all of these today"
3Transition (5 min): Bridge to content:
"What if I told you there's a framework for solving Y?"
Preview first module

Engagement Technique: Popcorn responses build participation; name-calling if needed ("Sarah, what's on your mind?")

BLOCK 3: MÓDULO 1 - CONCEPT + VIVO (00:30 - 01:15) | 45 min

Facilitator Role: Teaching + Co-creation

Objective: Introduce framework, demonstrate live, start building first output

Pasos:

1Concept intro (15 min):
Define the framework (e.g., Eisenhower, Ecosystem Map, etc.)
Why it matters (the why)
Show 2-3 examples (1 from their industry if possible)
Use visuals, not text-heavy slides
2Live demo (15 min):
Facilitator models the framework in real-time
E.g., for Eisenhower: "Let me take MY calendar and map it to urgent/important"
Narrate thinking out loud ("I'm putting this meeting here because...")
Invite questions mid-demo
3Group build (15 min):
Transition to tables: "Now it's your turn"
Provide template (canvas or digital)
Facilitator walks around, listens, nudges
Ask 1-2 groups to share what they have
Celebrate early progress

Energy Management: Watch for:

People zoning out? → ask direct question to re-engage
Too quiet? → tell story to re-energize
Too fast? → pause, check for understanding

Co-creation Principle: NEVER present finished product; co-build with group

BLOCK 4: DESCANSO (01:15 - 01:30) | 15 min

Facilitator Role: Stepping back

Pasos:

1Announce break (enthusiastically):
"We've earned a break. Back in 12 minutes sharp."
Point to coffee, bathrooms
2During break: Facilitator steps outside or sits quietly
Listen for energy level
Check if participants talking about workshop (good sign)
Recharge yourself
3Call people back: Stand up, clap hands, smile
"Alright, let's get back. You ready?"
Start promptly (builds trust in timing)

BLOCK 5: MÓDULO 2 - PROFUNDIDAD + TALLER (01:30 - 02:15) | 45 min

Facilitator Role: Teaching + group work

Objective: Go deeper, build more sophisticated version of tool

Pasos:

1Review (5 min):
Quickly recap what you built in Module 1
Show completed version (what they're building toward)
2Concept 2 (15 min):
Introduce next layer (e.g., prioritization, sequencing, etc.)
Show examples, stories, case studies
Connect to their context
3Group build v2 (20 min):
Return to tables
Build more complex version (add details, prioritize, sequence)
Asistente + Facilitador circulate, ask probing questions:
"Why did you rank that way?"
"What would change if you..."
"How will you know it's working?"
4Gallery walk (5 min) [if time allows]:
Groups stand by their canvas
Walk around, look at others' work
Celebrate different approaches

Facilitation Technique:

Use "I notice..." language (non-judgmental)
Ask questions, don't give answers
Celebrate effort, not just correctness

BLOCK 6: APLICACIÓN A CONTEXTO ESPECÍFICO (02:15 - 02:45) | 30 min

Facilitator Role: Coaching + Small group

Objective: Translate learning to their specific reality

Pasos:

1Context setting (5 min):
"Now let's make this real for YOUR world"
Share prompt: "What's one challenge you face right now that this framework could help with?"
2Small group work (15 min):
Arrange tables into 3-4 groups (mixed roles)
Each group gets 1 challenge question
Asistente facilitates each table (asking questions, probing deeper)
Main facilitador circulates, listens, nudges
3Group share (10 min):
Each group shares their insight (1-2 min per group)
Facilitator adds context, connects to frameworks
Celebrate insight, validate struggle

Engagement: This is where magic happens—real application of learning

BLOCK 7: COMMITMENT & HOJA DE RUTA (02:45 - 02:55) | 10 min

Facilitator Role: Coaching + accountability

Objective: Every person leaves with 3+ specific actions committed

Pasos:

1Action card introduction (1 min):
Hold up action card template
Explain: "You're going to write 3 things you'll do in next 2 weeks"
Make it specific, measurable, time-bound
2Silent reflection (5 min):
Everyone writes 3 actions (individually, not grouped)
Facilitator quiet, but available for Qs
Ops/Asistente capture photos of cards (data collection)
3Popcorn share (3 min):
Volunteers share 1 action (5-10 people if time)
Facilitator celebrates specificity
"I'm going to implement GTD in my calendar by Friday" ✓
NOT "I'll be more productive" ✗
4Accountability pairing (1 min):
"Find someone next to you who shares similar action"
Exchange contact info
"Check in with them in 2 weeks"

Tone: Energizing, hopeful, slightly urgent ("You CAN do this")

BLOCK 8: CIERRE & ENCUESTA NPS (02:55 - 03:00) | 5 min

Facilitator Role: Gratitude + Data collection

Pasos:

1Thank you (1 min):
Stand at front, look at group
"Thank you. Seriously. You showed up, you engaged, you built something real today."
Applause (lead with hands)
2NPS Survey (2 min):
Hold up NPS card (or show on screen)
"On scale 0-10, how likely are you to recommend this workshop?"
Collect cards (Asistente gathers immediately post-session)
Or: Use Typeform link on screen (QR code on exit)
3Closing words (2 min):
Share one final insight or belief
Remind of next steps (materials will be emailed, etc.)
Personal goodbye ("I'm here if you want to chat after")
4Dismiss:
Stand by door, high-five people as they leave
Collect any feedback verbally
Real-Time Facilitation Techniques ▼

Energy Management

Watch for these signals:

0-30 min: Usually high energy (fresh)
30-90 min: Possible dip (need story, activity change)
90-120 min: CRITICAL ZONE — post-break tiredness
120-180 min: Usually recover if content is engaging

Interventions:

Story: If energy drops, tell a story (45 sec, relevant, personal)
Activity change: Switch from lecture → group work → individual reflection
Movement: Stand up, stretch, walk around
Humor: Levity, not jokes (self-deprecating OK)
Urgency: "We have only X minutes left, so let's focus"

Handling Difficult Moments

ScenarioResponse
Dominant person talking too muchGently redirect: "Thanks [name]. Let's hear from someone else—[person], what are you thinking?"
Someone on their phoneLight comment: "I know emails are tempting, but let's be here together for 3 hours"
Skeptical person challenging ideaWelcome it: "Great question. That's exactly the kind of critical thinking we need"
Quiet person hasn't spokenDirect but gentle: "We haven't heard from this side of the table—what's on your mind?"
Technical difficultyPause, problem-solve quickly, or pivot: "Tech is being slow; let's do this on flip chart instead"
Participant gets emotionalPause, validate: "This stuff is real and matters. It's OK to feel." Offer break.

Psychological Safety Techniques

DO:

Use "we" language: "We're all figuring this out"
Validate all ideas: "I appreciate that perspective"
Normalize struggle: "This is hard; that's how we know it's working"
Celebrate questions: "Best question all day!"
Share your own mistakes: "I tried this and failed first, then..."

DON'T:

Dismiss ideas, even bad ones
Make fun of anyone
Rush people who are processing
Use jargon without explaining
Show frustration with pace
Documentation During Session ▼

Facilitator's Role

Timing keeper (Facilitador watches clock, nudges forward)
Energy manager (Facilitador reads room, adjusts)
Question fielder (Facilitador listens for themes, calls out patterns)

Asistente's Role

Photographer (Capture canvases, action cards, group moments)
Note taker (Write down key quotes, insights, questions)
Table facilitator (During small group work, ask probing questions)
Tech monitor (Ensure recording is going, WiFi stable)

Data Capture

During session, Asistente collects:

1Photos of completed canvases (2-3 angles per group)
2Attendance sheet (names, check-in)
3NPS responses (cards or digital)
4Key quotes (things people said that reveal insight)
5Questions asked (for future content evolution)

All data goes to shared Google Drive folder post-session.

Emergency Protocols (During Session) ▼

Emergency 1: Tech Failure

If projector/AV goes down:

1Pause gracefully: "Let's take a tech pause"
2Assess: Is it 5-min fix or 15+ min?
3If <5 min: "One moment, we'll be right back"
Asistente troubleshoots behind the scenes
Facilitator tells story or asks questions to fill time
4If >5 min: Pivot to whiteboard/flip chart
"Let's go analog; sometimes that's better anyway"
Draw diagrams by hand
Continue content unchanged

Emergency 2: Low Attendance (<30%)

1Acknowledge: "We have a smaller group today, which is great—more intimate"
2Adapt: Add more Q&A, deeper conversation
3Proceed: Don't cancel or rush; quality over quantity
4Document: Note in post-workshop report (pattern analysis)

Emergency 3: Participant Gets Sick/Injured

1Pause session
2Asistente helps participant (water, bathroom, first aid if needed)
3If serious: Call 911 or emergency contact
4If minor: Resume once settled
5Follow up post-workshop (empathy)

Emergency 4: Extremely Disruptive Participant

1First: Politely redirect:
"I appreciate your passion; let's chat during break about this"
Move on with content
2Second offense: Private conversation during break:
"Hey, I want to understand what's bothering you"
Listen, validate, reset expectations
"Can we come back together for the next 90 minutes?"
3If won't cooperate: Ask them to step out:
"This isn't working. Let's reconvene offline after workshop"
Proceed with rest of group
Escalate to Ops post-workshop
Post-Session (T+0 to T+5 min) ▼

Immediate Actions

1Stop recording (Asistente)
2Collect NPS cards (Asistente)
3Take group photo (Asistente)
4Thank everyone (Facilitator at door)
5Collect action cards (Asistente scans/photos)
6Hand out any materials (Ops)
7Availability for questions (Facilitator stays 10 min post)

Facilitator Debrief (Facilitator + Asistente, 15 min)

While memory is fresh:

What went well?
What would you do differently?
Any insights about group or content?
Any questions that came up for future evolution?
Any technical/logistical issues?
EDGE CASES Casos Borde (Decisiones en Vivo) ▼

Caso 1: Participante dominante habla 60% del tiempo

Signal: Una persona monopoliza Q&A, otros se sienten silenciados

Decision: Minute 45, usar redirect: "[Name], gracias. Escuchemos a alguien que no ha hablado todavía. [Other person], ¿qué piensas?"

Justificación: Psychological safety requiere que todos puedan hablar. Interrumpir dominancia early previene group shutdown.

Caso 2: 3 participantes claramente perdidos (confused faces min 60)

Signal: Cuerpo lenguaje sugiere desconexión (sin notas, mirando al vacio)

Decision: Minute 70, mini-reset: "Pause. Let me reframe this more simply..." + ejemplo concreto de su mundo.

Justificación: Si 15%+ se pierden, mensaje no está clear. Re-teach breve = mejor que proceder.

Caso 3: Tech falla completa (proyector muerte) min 45

Signal: No image, no sound

Decision: "Tech pause. Let's go analog." Facilitador dibuja en whiteboard los conceptos pendientes, narración en vivo.

Justificación: Workshop es sobre IDEAS, no slides. Continuamos sin tech.

Caso 4: Participante emocional (ojos llorosos) min 120

Signal: Algo en contenido toca personalmente

Decision: Pause, validar: "This stuff is real and matters. It's OK to feel." Oferta de break. Luego continuar.

Justificación: Emotion = engagement profundo. Normalize, don't pathologize.

Caso 5: Solo 60% asistencia cuando empezó workshop (no-shows no avisaron)

Signal: 12 de 20 presentes

Decision: Proceder. Ejecutar mismo workshop, documentar no-show rate. Después investigar patrón con Account Manager.

Justificación: Calidad educativa no cambia con grupo pequeño (ventaja: más diálogo). No cancelar = respeto a quienes SÍ vinieron.

ANTI-PATTERNS Anti-Patterns (QUÉ EVITAR) ▼
NO improvisar contenido en vivo (preparación es responsabilidad Prep phase)
NO leer slides word-by-word (elocución muere, engagement cae)
NO ignorar participante perdido (esperando que se recupere solo)
NO responder directamente preguntas off-scope; SIEMPRE defer con link/contacto
NO hacer timing como "lo importante es profundidad", NO respetes breaks (tiempo = contrato)
NO faltar facilitador asistente (sin asistente: data capture imposible, ejecución degradada)

Facilitator Energy Management (Personal) ▼

Before workshop:

Sleep well (7+ hours)
Light exercise (15 min walk)
Eat something (don't go in hungry)
Review briefing doc one more time
Deep breathing (5 min quiet)

During workshop:

Drink water
Use bathroom during break
Stand most of time (energy transfer)
Make eye contact with each person 1x minimum
Use your voice variation (not monotone)
Pace the room (don't stay in one spot)

After workshop:

Debrief with Asistente
Drink more water
Eat something
30 min alone time (you gave a lot)
